My Rusty Submarine – Patch the leaks, keep the old sub moving

Nothing on this submarine works for long. My Rusty Submarine puts you in charge of an aging, unreliable vessel and asks you to keep it running as it travels. Leaks spring, systems break and emergencies stack up. The game is about triage: fixing the right thing first, and sharing the work when a partner is aboard.

The basics of My Rusty Submarine

The submarine moves through the water while problems keep appearing on board. Leaks let water in, broken systems stop working, and each issue needs attention before it turns into a bigger one. You move around the sub to handle repairs and keep it going. With a partner, the load splits in two. The goal is to keep the vessel running through the voyage.

Getting started

  • Learn where each station and system sits inside the submarine.
  • Fix leaks quickly so they do not pile up.
  • Split the sub into zones when you play with a partner.
  • Call out new problems the moment you see them.

Tips from experience

Stay ahead of trouble. Small problems are quick to fix and become hard ones when left alone. Keep moving between stations instead of waiting at one. In co-op, one player can watch for new faults while the other repairs. Agree on who handles what before things go wrong, because there is little time to discuss it during an emergency.

Who should play it

Best with a partner who enjoys frantic, cooperative tasks. Solo play works but feels busier. The Unity WebGL build can be heavy on older computers, so load times and performance may vary.
