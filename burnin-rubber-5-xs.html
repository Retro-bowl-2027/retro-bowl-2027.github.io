Burnin' Rubber 5 XS Online

Burnin' Rubber 5 XS – Race hard and keep both weapons loaded

Racing with weapons bolted on. XformGames' 2020 entry in the Burnin' Rubber series puts you behind the wheel on a range of tracks with a pair of weapons ready to slow anyone in your way. It runs in Unity WebGL with sharp visuals and quick action, but the fundamentals of racing still matter most.

The basics of Burnin' Rubber 5 XS

The arrow keys handle throttle, braking and steering. Z and X fire your two weapons, which can damage or slow rival cars. The goal in each race is to finish ahead of the field. Clean driving keeps your speed high, while crashes and spins lose you places no weapon can win back. Use your arsenal to protect a lead or close a gap.

Controls

  • Arrow Keys – drive
  • Z – weapon 1
  • X – weapon 2

Getting started

  • Drive a clean first lap to learn the corners before using weapons.
  • Brake before corners rather than in them to keep the car stable.
  • Fire at rivals directly ahead, where a shot is most likely to land.
  • Save one weapon for defending a lead late in the race.

Tips from experience

Racing line first, weapons second. Wide entries into corners and early acceleration out of them gain more time than any shot. Fire when a rival is directly in your path and close enough to hit. Do not swerve to aim; it costs speed. Learn where each track has long straights and plan your attacks there.

Who should play it

Suits players who like arcade racers with a combat twist. The visuals are polished for a browser game. Pure racing fans may find the weapons distracting, and slower computers may struggle with the 3D.
