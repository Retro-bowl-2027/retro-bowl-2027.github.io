The Battle Online

The Battle – Build towers along the path and hold

Enemies march along a fixed path, and your towers decide whether they arrive. The Battle is a Flash tower defense game with the classic setup: choose where to build, what to upgrade and how to spend your money. Cover the whole route and save some funds for the waves that come later.

The basics of The Battle

Build towers on the open ground beside the enemy path. Each one fires at anything that comes within range. Every defeated enemy pays out, and that money buys more towers or upgrades for the ones you have. Waves grow larger and tougher as the level goes on. Stop every enemy before it reaches the end to survive the level.

Getting started

  • Place your first towers at a bend where enemies pass twice.
  • Cover the full length of the path before upgrading.
  • Upgrade the towers that cover the longest stretch of path.
  • Keep a cash reserve for emergencies in later waves.

Tips from experience

Placement first, upgrades second. A well placed tower outperforms an upgraded one in a bad spot. Watch which enemies slip through and adjust. Do not spend everything early; later waves need answers. Plan for the final waves from the start.

Who should play it

Good for tower defense fans who enjoy classic Flash games. The rules are easy to learn. The older visuals and simple systems may feel dated next to modern defense games.
