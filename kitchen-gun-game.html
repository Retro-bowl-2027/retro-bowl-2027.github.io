Kitchen Gun Game – Snap shots at targets around the kitchen

Ash K set this shooter somewhere unexpected: a kitchen. Kitchen Gun Game is a quick HTML5 aim test where targets show up around the room for a brief moment, and you have to hit each one before it slips away. The location is played for laughs, and a round is over so fast that a retry is always tempting.

The basics of Kitchen Gun Game

Targets flash up in different spots and vanish after a short time. Line up the cursor and click before they disappear. Quick, accurate hits push your score up; wild shots throw away time and chances. Each round lasts only moments, so every target matters. Beat your previous score to call the round a success.

Getting started

  • Rest the cursor in the middle of the screen between targets.
  • Click once your aim is settled, never while still sweeping.
  • Hit the target that has been visible longest before the fresh ones.
  • Run another round straight away to keep your rhythm warm.

Tips from experience

Hits matter more than haste. A miss costs more than a slightly slower hit. Glide the mouse and stop before you click. Notice where targets like to appear and keep the cursor near those spots. A loose grip prevents overshooting. A couple of warm-up rounds sharpen your aim before a serious attempt.

Who should play it

A light aim test with a silly setting. Rounds are over in moments. There is little depth, so treat it as a brief distraction between other games.
