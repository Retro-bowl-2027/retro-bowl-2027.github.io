Soccer Random – Floppy players, one button, a new pitch every round

Rubbery players flail across the field and nobody knows which way the ball will go next. Soccer Random is a two-player soccer game where each side has a single button. Pressing it makes your floppy team leap and swing a leg. The pitch and conditions shift between rounds, so the match keeps changing under your feet.

The basics of Soccer Random

Both players share a keyboard, each with one button. Tap it and your team hops and kicks in whatever direction its loose limbs allow. Use the timing of each press to push the ball toward the opposing net. Between rounds the arena, the ball or the conditions may change, and every change asks for a new approach. Reach the winning score first.

Getting started

  • Spend a round learning which way your players kick when you press.
  • Press just before the ball reaches your players rather than spamming.
  • Look over the new arena at the start of each round.
  • Play against a friend at the same keyboard for the best matches.

Tips from experience

Timing beats mashing. One well-timed press sends the ball forward; ten panicked ones send your players into a heap. Adapt quickly to each new setup. Let the strange bounces work for you. Stay patient, since chaos creates as many chances as it ruins.

Who should play it

Ideal for friends who want quick, goofy matches. The random setups keep rounds fresh. Players who want controlled, skill-based soccer may find the chaos frustrating.
