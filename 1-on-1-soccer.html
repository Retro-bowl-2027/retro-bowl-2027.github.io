1 on 1 Soccer Online

1 on 1 Soccer – Two players, one ball, a short clock

Strip football down to one player per side and a pocket-sized pitch and you get this 2014 browser game by Adam Wardle. There are no teammates to pass to and no formation to set. You chase the ball, knock it at the other net and hope to lead when time expires.

The basics of 1 on 1 Soccer

Each match is a race against the clock. Arrow keys or WASD steer your player, and the ball goes wherever your body shoves it. The ball bounces hard and loose, so it rarely goes where a real kick would send it. Score more goals than your rival before the timer ends. Odd rebounds and lucky deflections decide many points, which keeps every match unpredictable.

Controls

  • Arrow / WASD Keys – move

Getting started

  • Play one match just to learn how high and far the ball bounces.
  • Guard your goal side whenever your rival controls the ball.
  • Get behind the ball before touching it, so it rolls toward the opposing net.
  • Watch the timer and protect a lead in the final seconds.

Tips from experience

Patience wins more points than speed. Rushing every loose ball leaves your goal open to a single bounce. Let the ball come down, then strike when its path is predictable. Hit it at an angle so it skips past your opponent rather than into them. When you are ahead late, sit back and clear the ball away from your goal; the clock is on your side.

Who should play it

Suits anyone who wants a full match in a couple of minutes. Kids pick it up instantly. Players looking for tactics, squads or a league will run out of things to do quickly.
