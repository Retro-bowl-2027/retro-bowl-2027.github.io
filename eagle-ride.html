Eagle Ride – Swoop past every obstacle in the sky

An eagle cuts through the sky at speed, and everything in front of it is something to avoid. Aron Sommer's 2019 Unity game keeps the controls to two directions: dash left or dash right. The longer you stay airborne, the quicker the hazards arrive and the narrower the safe paths between them become.

The basics of Eagle Ride

The eagle flies forward on its own. Tap left or right, on the arrows or A and D, to dash sideways into a new gap. Each dash shifts the bird sharply to one side, so you choose the direction and the moment. Hitting an obstacle ends the flight. Your score reflects how far you get before that happens.

Controls

  • Arrow / WASD Keys – dash Left / Right

Getting started

  • Watch the sky ahead and spot gaps before they arrive.
  • Dash early rather than at the last instant.
  • Drift back toward the middle when the path is clear.
  • Stay calm as speed rises; the controls never change.

Tips from experience

Plan two dashes ahead. Obstacles often come in sequences that need a left followed by a right, and reacting to each separately is too slow at high speed. Avoid dashing twice in a row unless you have to; extra movement creates new risks. Breathing steadily sounds silly, but it keeps your timing smooth.

Who should play it

Good for players who want a quick, simple reflex game with pleasant visuals. It is easy to learn. The two-input design limits depth, and runs can feel similar after a while.
