Gunblood – Wait for the signal, then draw first

Two gunslingers stand in the dust, hands ready, waiting for the signal. Wolf Games' Flash-era Western shooter is built on that frozen moment. When the call comes, aim and fire faster and more accurately than your rival. Move before the signal and you lose the round. Hesitate after it and you lose something worse.

The basics of Gunblood

Each duel starts with a pause. When the signal appears, aim with the mouse and click to fire. Shooting too early counts against you. The quickest accurate shot wins the duel, while a fast miss hands your opponent a free chance. Beat each gunslinger to face a quicker one, until you reach the toughest shooters in the game.

Getting started

  • Keep your cursor still and close to where your opponent stands.
  • Watch for the signal and ignore everything else on screen.
  • Aim at the center of your opponent for the surest hit.
  • Take a breath and reset your hand before each new duel.

Tips from experience

Speed matters, but accuracy wins. A fast miss gives your rival a free shot, while a slightly slower hit ends the duel. Rest your cursor near your opponent before the signal so you only need a small movement. Do not anticipate the signal; react to it. Later opponents draw faster, so smooth, practiced motions matter more than raw speed.

Who should play it

Fits anyone who enjoys pure reaction tests with style. Each duel lasts seconds. The concept is narrow, so it is best in short bursts rather than long sessions.
