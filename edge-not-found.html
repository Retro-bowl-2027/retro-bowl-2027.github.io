Edge Not Found – Puzzles where the screen edge is part of the board

The name riffs on a browser error message, and the puzzles riff on something players usually ignore: the borders of the screen. Auroriax's 2020 puzzle game asks you to rethink what happens at the edge of a level. Walls may not be walls, and leaving one side of the map might be the fastest route somewhere else.

The basics of Edge Not Found

Each level is a compact grid with a goal. Your task is to discover how its boundaries behave and use that to reach the solution. Some edges may connect to others, so moving off one side brings you back in from another. Early stages teach the rules, and later ones combine them into puzzles that demand careful planning.

Getting started

  • Walk into every edge on the first levels to learn what happens.
  • Sketch the level on paper if you need to track where edges lead.
  • Plan your route on the wrapped map rather than the visible one.
  • Restart a level when you reach a position you cannot escape.

Tips from experience

Treat the edges as connections, not limits. Once you know how a level wraps, picture the map as a loop and plan around that. Many puzzles hide a short path through the border that looks impossible at first glance. Slow down on later levels and test each idea in your head before moving. Small grids mean every move counts.

Who should play it

Fits players who enjoy clever, rule-bending puzzles with a clean look. Levels are compact. Players who want action or lots of hand-holding may find it abstract.
