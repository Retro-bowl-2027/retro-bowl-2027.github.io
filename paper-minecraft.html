Paper Minecraft – The block adventure, flattened into paper

Griffpatch built Paper Minecraft on Scratch in December 2014, and it became one of that platform's best known projects. It presses the block adventure flat into a side view with a cut-paper look. Dig, gather, make tools and put up walls, all in two dimensions. This is a fan project; the original game and its name belong to their owners.

The basics of Paper Minecraft

The world is a side-view slice of terrain. Break blocks to collect wood, stone and ore. Turn those materials into tools and items that dig faster and let you build sturdier structures. A shelter keeps you safe while you plan the next trip. No fixed goal exists, but progress depends on gathering efficiently.

Getting started

  • Chop the trees near spawn for your first materials.
  • Open the crafting screen often to see what you can make.
  • Dig sideways into hillsides rather than straight down.
  • Build a small room near spawn before heading far away.

Tips from experience

Check the crafting menu every time you collect something new; new recipes become possible as your supplies grow. Dig into hills to find ore without falling far. Keep a few spare blocks to wall off danger. Save your world if the version allows it, so a long session is not lost.

Who should play it

Suits fans of the block game who want a lighter 2D version. It shows how much a Scratch project can do. As a fan project, it has fewer features than the original.
