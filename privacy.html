Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This page answers the questions people usually have about data on Retro-Bowl-2027 (retro-bowl-2027.github.io). Short version: we have no accounts and no forms, so we never ask who you are. The data that does exist comes from our host, from Google's ad and analytics tools, and from the games themselves.

What gets recorded when I open a page?

Our hosting provider logs each request. A log line holds an IP address, the browser and operating system, the device type, the address you came from, the page requested and a timestamp. We read these logs to fix errors, spot abuse and see which pages people actually use.

Why do I see ads, and how are they chosen?

Ads on Retro-Bowl-2027 come from Google AdSense. Google, acting as a third-party vendor, places cookies in your browser and uses them to pick ads based on your earlier visits to this site and to other websites. With those advertising cookies, Google and its partners can show you ads based on what you looked at here and/or on other sites across the Internet.

Three ways to limit this:

  • Switch off personalized ads at adssettings.google.com.
  • Opt out of other participating ad vendors at aboutads.info/choices.
  • Read how Google treats data from partner sites like this one at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland get a consent prompt first. Nothing personalized runs until you answer it, and you can reopen it later to change your answer.

Do you track what I do on the site?

Only in aggregate. Google Analytics counts page views, time on page, the type of device and a rough location. We use the totals to decide which games to keep and which pages need work. Block it completely with Google's Analytics opt-out add-on.

What about the games?

Each game loads from its own server inside a frame. It may write cookies or local storage of its own, usually to keep your progress or settings. That storage belongs to the game's developer, and their privacy terms apply to it. Nothing loads until you press play.

Is the site meant for kids?

It is a general-audience site. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. A parent who thinks their child sent us personal details can email us and we will erase them.

What rights do I have?

Under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA you may be able to see, correct or delete personal data, limit or object to how it is used, or withdraw consent. Retro-Bowl-2027 does not sell or share personal information for money. Email contact@retro-bowl-2027.github.io with your request.

How long is data kept?

Server logs are deleted once they are no longer needed for security or debugging. Google keeps its data under its own retention settings. Every page is served over HTTPS.

Will this page change?

Yes, when our tools or the rules change. The date at the top tells you when. Questions go to contact@retro-bowl-2027.github.io.
