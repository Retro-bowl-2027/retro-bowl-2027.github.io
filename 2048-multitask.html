2048 Multitask – Same sliding rules, a bigger finish line

Cirulli's merging puzzle returns with a heavier workload. The basic rules have not moved: slide the tiles, combine equal numbers and keep the grid from filling up. What changes is how far you are asked to go. The target sits at 2048 and can stretch to 3072 for players who want more, which means more merges, more planning and less room for a careless swipe.

The basics of 2048 Multitask

Every key press slides all tiles one way. Matching tiles that meet merge into one worth their sum. A new tile spawns after each move, so every turn takes a cell away unless you merge something. You succeed when you hit the target and lose when the board locks with no legal merge. Longer goals punish small mistakes that a normal game might forgive.

Controls

  • Arrow / WASD Keys – move Number Tiles

Getting started

  • Decide which target you are chasing before your first move.
  • Anchor your highest tile in one corner and do not let it drift.
  • Stick to two main directions and use a third sparingly.
  • Slow down once the board is more than half full.

Tips from experience

Over a long game, tidiness beats speed. Keep the tiles in a descending chain so merges cascade into each other. Avoid leaving a lone small tile behind a big one; it blocks the chain for dozens of moves. If your corner tile gets knocked loose, rebuild calmly instead of swiping at random. Short breaks help, because fatigue causes most late errors.

Who should play it

Aimed at 2048 regulars who have beaten the standard goal and want a longer test. The rules are identical, so there is no learning curve. If plain 2048 already bores you, a bigger number will not fix that.
