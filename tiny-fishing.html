Tiny Fishing Online

Tiny Fishing – Drop the hook deep, reel the ocean in

Line goes down, fish come up, cash rolls in. Tiny Fishing is an idle fishing game built on a two-part cast. On the way down you weave around fish to reach as deep as your line allows. On the way back up you steer straight into them, hooking as many as you can carry. Every haul pays for a better rig.

The basics of Tiny Fishing

Each cast has two halves. During the descent, steer the hook sideways to avoid touching fish, because a bite stops the drop early. Once it bottoms out, the hook rises and every fish it touches is caught, up to your limit. Sell the catch for money. Spend that money on a longer line, a larger catch limit and similar improvements, then cast again.

Getting started

  • Steer clear of fish on the descent so the hook reaches full depth.
  • Sweep into crowded schools on the rise to fill the line.
  • Put your first earnings into a longer line.
  • Raise the catch limit so each trip can hold more fish.

Tips from experience

Depth equals value. The deepest water holds the best-paying fish, so line length is usually the upgrade that matters most early. On the rise, sweep side to side through the thickest schools. Keep your catch limit in step with your depth, or you will reach rich water without room to carry it.

Who should play it

Good for players who like relaxed progression games. Each cast is quick. Long-term play becomes repetitive, so it works best as a casual pastime.
