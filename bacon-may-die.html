Bacon May Die Online

Bacon May Die – A strip of bacon fights its way through

The hero is a strip of bacon, and it is very angry. SnoutUp Games plays the joke for all it is worth, then backs it up with combat that takes skill. Stages fill with enemies, and survival depends on timing your hits, using special moves at the right moment and never getting caught in a crowd.

The basics of Bacon May Die

You move through stages and fight enemies with regular attacks and shooting, while special moves deal heavier damage or clear space around you. Foes arrive from several sides, so spacing matters. Clear each wave to move on. Damage drains your health, and running out ends the attempt. The pace is fast, but fights reward a plan more than button mashing.

Getting started

  • Learn your basic attack and special move on the first wave.
  • Keep enemies on one side of you whenever possible.
  • Save special moves for when several enemies close in at once.
  • Keep moving between attacks so foes cannot surround you.

Tips from experience

Mashing gets you surrounded. Hit, step back, hit again. Watch enemy wind-ups and dodge before the blow lands, then punish the opening. Specials are strongest against groups, so do not waste them on a single weak foe. Use ranged attacks to thin a crowd before it arrives. When a stage goes badly, focus on the most dangerous enemy type first.

Who should play it

A good pick for players who enjoy quick, silly brawlers with real timing. The humor lands fast. It is a one-note joke, so the appeal rests on how much you enjoy the fighting itself.
