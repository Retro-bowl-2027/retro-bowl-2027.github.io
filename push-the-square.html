Push The Square Online

Push The Square – Push the block, grow the numbers

One square, one button and a counter that keeps climbing. Push The Square is a Unity WebGL clicker built around a single act repeated thousands of times. Every shove of the block pays out. The payout buys boosts, some for each shove and some that keep paying while your hands rest. Make the counter climb faster.

The basics of Push The Square

Each click shoves the square and drops points into your total. The shop sells two kinds of boosts: ones that make every shove worth more, and ones that pay out on a timer without any clicking. Prices climb as you buy, while your income scales up to match. Picking the next purchase is the main decision. Keep shoving and spending to reach bigger totals.

Getting started

  • Click steadily at first to afford the early upgrades.
  • Buy an automatic income upgrade as soon as possible.
  • Compare upgrade prices against how much they add.
  • Check back after a while and spend what you earned.

Tips from experience

Value matters more than price. A cheap upgrade that adds little may be worse than saving for a better one. Automate early so income grows without effort. Keep a balance between click power and passive income. Spend earnings quickly so they start paying back.

Who should play it

Suits players who like minimal clickers with a clean look. It is easy to drop in and out. Anyone wanting more than number growth will find it thin.
