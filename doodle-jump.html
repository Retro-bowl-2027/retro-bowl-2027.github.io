Doodle Jump – Bounce higher, and never miss a platform

A four-legged green doodle bounces endlessly upward on a sheet of graph paper. Lima Sky's 2009 hit, one of the biggest mobile games of its era, gives you one job: guide each bounce onto the next platform and keep climbing. Miss and you fall off the bottom of the screen. Your score is how high you got.

The basics of Doodle Jump

The character jumps automatically each time it lands. You steer it left and right, and leaving one side of the screen brings it back on the other. Platforms vary: some move, some crumble after one touch and some vanish. Springs, propeller hats and jetpacks launch you higher. Monsters and black holes end the run on contact, unless you shoot the monsters first or land on top of them.

Getting started

  • Steer gently; small adjustments keep you over the platforms.
  • Use the screen wrap to reach platforms on the far side quickly.
  • Grab springs and jetpacks whenever they appear for free height.
  • Avoid brown cracked platforms, which break when you land.

Tips from experience

Look up, not down. Spot the next two or three platforms while you are still rising, and steer toward them before you start falling. Wrapping around the screen edge is often faster than crossing the middle. Approach monsters from above or shoot them early. When the platform spacing widens, choose safe landings over risky boosts.

Who should play it

A classic for any age and a perfect quick-break game. The controls are minimal. The core loop never changes, so it shines in short bursts rather than long sessions.
