1010 Deluxe – Fill a line, clear a line, repeat

Three shapes, one board, no time limit. This decorative take on the classic 1010 puzzle keeps the rules as simple as they should be. Drag pieces onto the grid. Complete a full row or column and it clears. Run out of space for the shapes in your hand and the game is over.

The basics of 1010 Deluxe

Pieces arrive a few at a time, and you must place all of them before the next set appears. Shapes cannot be turned, so you work with exactly what you are given. Each completed row or column disappears and adds to your score. There is no clock. The only enemy is a crowded board with no space left for the shape you hold.

Controls

  • Drag – move Pieces
  • Drop – drop Pieces

Getting started

  • Place large pieces first while the board still has room for them.
  • Fill rows along one edge before you build in the middle.
  • Keep a three-by-three area empty for the biggest square block.
  • Try to finish two lines with a single piece whenever you can.

Tips from experience

Most games end because of one awkward piece, usually the long bar or the big square. Plan for both. Keep one full lane open for the bar and one clear square area at all times. Do not chase points by leaving near-complete lines everywhere; half-filled rows across the whole board leave no room to recover. Clear lines early and often.

Who should play it

Ideal for short breaks and for anyone who enjoys tidy spatial puzzles. The pace is entirely yours. Experienced 1010 players will find nothing new in the rules, only a fresh coat of paint.
