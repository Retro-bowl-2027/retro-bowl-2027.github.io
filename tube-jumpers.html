Tube Jumpers – Hop at the right instant and outlast the rest

Michael Eichler's Tube Jumpers, a Unity WebGL arcade game from June 2016, is all about the instant you leave the ground. Obstacles sweep in, you hop, and either you clear them or you are out. The tempo keeps climbing. With friends joining in, it becomes a loud little contest over who can survive the longest.

The basics of Tube Jumpers

Hazards keep sweeping through, and a well-timed hop carries you over each one. Leave too early or too late and you are knocked out of the round. As the round goes on, everything speeds up and the window for a clean hop narrows. When several people play, survival becomes a contest, and the final jumper standing claims the round.

Getting started

  • Let a couple of hazards pass while you study their speed.
  • Hop on a steady rhythm instead of reacting at the last second.
  • Keep your eyes on the next hazard as the tempo rises.
  • Invite friends in for a livelier, more competitive round.

Tips from experience

Rhythm matters more than reflexes. Hop at the last safe moment, not as soon as you see trouble, so you land before the next hazard. Match your timing to the pattern rather than reacting to each one fresh. In group play, ignore what the others are doing and watch your own character.

Who should play it

Good for groups of friends and quick arcade breaks. Rounds are short and lively. On your own the appeal fades faster, since much of the fun comes from competing.
