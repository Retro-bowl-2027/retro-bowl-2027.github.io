Matrix Rampage – Shoot first, keep moving, never stand still

Built by Suriyathept and released in late 2006, Matrix Rampage is a fast run and gun shooter from the Flash years. Enemies arrive in groups and from several sides at once. The goal is to fight through each area alive. Good aim helps, but reading the screen and moving at the right moment matter more.

The basics of Matrix Rampage

Each area is a fight against a steady stream of attackers. You move, shoot and slip out of the line of fire while the threats close in. Enemies do not wait their turn, so one careless stop can cost a big chunk of health. Clear the area to push forward. The pressure rises as groups get larger and attacks come from more directions.

Getting started

  • Learn the movement and firing keys on the first screen before the shooting starts.
  • Stay near the middle of the play area so you can escape either way.
  • Shoot the closest threat first, then turn to the next one.
  • Keep moving between shots instead of standing still to aim.

Tips from experience

Never fight in a corner. A corner removes half your escape routes. Watch the edges of the screen, since new enemies often enter from there. Fire in short bursts while you reposition. When health runs low, back away from the crowd and thin it out from range. Restart quickly after a loss and replay the same area until its rhythm feels familiar.

Who should play it

Fans of old browser shooters will feel at home here. It suits players who like short, intense fights and quick restarts. The presentation shows its age, and anyone looking for story or deep progression will find little of either.
