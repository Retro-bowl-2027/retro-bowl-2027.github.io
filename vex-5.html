Vex 5 Online

Vex 5 – More acts to master, more ways to fail

Another sequel, another set of acts to conquer. Vex 5 adds new obstacles that test your movement skills, building on what the series has taught you. Reach checkpoints, collect coins and push through to the end of each act. Precision and patience remain the tools of the trade.

The basics of Vex 5

Each act is a long sequence of hazards. Move, jump, slide and dodge to get past spikes, blades and traps. Checkpoints save your progress inside each act, so a mistake costs only a section. Coins reward careful exploration of side paths. Finish acts to unlock more and work toward completing the whole game.

Controls

  • Up Arrow – jump
  • Down Arrow – slide / Dodge
  • Left / Right Arrows – move Left / Right
  • Note – wASD keys also work

Getting started

  • Warm up on the first act even if you know earlier Vex games.
  • Find each checkpoint and treat it as a safe practice spot.
  • Study new obstacles before trying to rush through them.
  • Collect coins only when the route to them is safe.

Tips from experience

Build on what you know. Moves from earlier games still work, but new hazards need fresh timing. Practice hard sections from checkpoints until they feel automatic. Watch for moving traps and wait for safe windows. Stay patient, since rushing in Vex usually means repeating a section.

Who should play it

Suits series fans who want more challenge. The difficulty is steady but demanding. Players new to Vex may want to start with an earlier game.
