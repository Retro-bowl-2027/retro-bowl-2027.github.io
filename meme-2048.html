Meme 2048 – Merge memes, keep the big tile cornered

Same rules as Gabriele Cirulli's 2048, different faces on the tiles. This 3kh0 version swaps the numbers for internet meme pictures, so each merge reveals a new image instead of a bigger number. Slide the board, combine matching pairs and reach the final picture before the grid locks up.

The basics of Meme 2048

The board is a four by four grid. Every move slides all tiles in one direction, and two matching tiles that collide become one tile of the next level. A new tile appears after each move. You lose when the grid is full and no slide can merge anything. The trick is remembering the order of the pictures, since you cannot read values off the tiles.

Getting started

  • Play a few slow games to learn which meme follows which in the sequence.
  • Pick one corner and keep your highest tile there for the whole game.
  • Use only two or three directions for most moves to protect that corner.
  • Fill the edge row beside the corner with tiles in descending order.

Tips from experience

Avoid the direction that pulls your big tile out of its corner. When you are forced to use it, slide back at once. Build chains along the edge so one merge sets up the next. Do not chase every possible merge; leave space open in the middle. When you lose track of the order, pause and compare the images before moving.

Who should play it

Good for 2048 players who want a lighter look, and for meme fans who like a quick puzzle. The pictures add a memory step the number version lacks, which some players enjoy and others find annoying.
