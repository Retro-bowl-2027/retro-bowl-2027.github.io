Tag – Chase, flee and swap roles on the platforms

The playground game moves onto platforms. Tag puts runners on a stage of ledges and gaps where one player is it and everyone else is trying very hard not to be. Being caught flips the roles. Clever routes, sudden direction changes and good use of the level's layout keep you out of reach, or bring a target within it.

The basics of Tag

Each round, one player chases and the others flee. Run and jump across the platforms to stay away from the chaser, or close the gap when you are the one doing the chasing. A tag passes the role on. The level's shape is your main tool: ledges, drops and shortcuts can save you or trap you. Spend the least time as the chaser to come out ahead.

Getting started

  • Run a lap of the level early to learn its shortcuts and dead ends.
  • Keep moving when you are not the chaser; standing still invites a tag.
  • Cut across platforms when chasing instead of following the same path.
  • Use drops and jumps to change direction faster than your pursuer.

Tips from experience

Avoid corners. A dead end is the easiest place to get caught. Watch the chaser, not your feet. When you are it, aim for where the runner is heading, since direct pursuit rarely works. Learn which jumps are faster than walking around, and use them both ways.

Who should play it

Great for friends who want quick, playful competition. Rounds are short and chaotic. The idea is simple, so players looking for depth or progression may tire of it quickly.
