Scrap Metal 3 Online

Scrap Metal 3 – Ram, survive and be the last car running

Rusted wrecks circle each other in a dirt bowl, and only one engine will still be running at the end. Scrap Metal 3 is a Unity WebGL demolition derby about dishing out damage while dodging it. Broadside impacts do the most harm, so angles matter more than top speed. Survive, cash in, then patch up and improve your car for the next bout.

The basics of Scrap Metal 3

Every bout drops you into a bowl with a pack of opponents. Circle, pick a target and slam into it, ideally from the side. Meanwhile, keep your own flanks away from incoming bumpers. When only your car is still going, the bout is yours. Winnings pay for repairs and better parts before the following round.

Getting started

  • Learn how your car accelerates and turns before attacking.
  • Aim for rivals' sides instead of their front bumpers.
  • Keep moving so other cars cannot line up a clean hit.
  • Spend rewards on repairs first, then buy upgrades with what is left.

Tips from experience

Pick your fights. Let rivals damage each other, then finish the weakest. Avoid getting trapped against walls. Hit at angles to deal more damage. Protect your car's sides; a strong side hit can end your round quickly. Watch your damage between rounds.

Who should play it

Suits players who enjoy car combat and destruction. Rounds are short and chaotic. The WebGL build may run slowly on older devices, and the derby format offers limited variety.
