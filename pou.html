Pou – Feed it, wash it, play with it, repeat

A small round creature depends on you. This Pou is an unofficial browser take on the virtual pet idea. Feed it, keep it clean, let it sleep and play mini games to keep it happy. The appeal is the routine itself. Small care tasks add up, and your pet grows over time.

The basics of Pou

Your pet has needs that drop over time: hunger, cleanliness, energy and fun. Feed it, wash it, put it to bed when it is tired, and play mini games to cheer it up. Mini games can earn coins, which buy food, items and decorations. Keep every need topped up and your pet stays content.

Getting started

  • Check your pet's needs as soon as you open the game.
  • Feed it when hunger gets low, rather than waiting until empty.
  • Play a mini game to raise its mood and earn some coins.
  • Let it rest when its energy is nearly gone.

Tips from experience

Little and often works best. Short check-ins keep every need from falling too low. Spend coins on food first, then on extras. Try all the mini games to find the ones that pay best for you. Keep a routine and your pet will thrive.

Who should play it

Suits younger players and anyone who enjoys calm care routines. There is no failure pressure. As an unofficial version, features may differ from the well known mobile app.
