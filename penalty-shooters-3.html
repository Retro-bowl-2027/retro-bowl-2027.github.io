Penalty Shooters 3 – The shootout series returns with new competitions

Cleaner presentation and new competitions arrive in Penalty Shooters 3. The heart of the game is unchanged: take turns shooting and saving until one side wins. Tournaments raise the stakes round by round. Choose shot direction and power, and read your opponent when it is your turn in goal.

The basics of Penalty Shooters 3

Matches alternate between attack and defense. On attack, pick where to shoot and how hard. On defense, anticipate the opponent's aim and move to block it. Win shootouts to progress through tournament rounds, where rivals get tougher and every miss costs more. The format keeps each kick meaningful, and a single save can swing a whole round.

Getting started

  • Play your first matches slowly to learn the timing of each kick.
  • Vary shot direction between rounds so keepers cannot read you.
  • Watch where the opponent aims and react early on defense.
  • Enter a tournament once you feel steady in both roles.

Tips from experience

Think like the keeper when you shoot. Ask which side you would guard, then aim for the other. Do not rely on power alone; placement wins more goals. When defending, look for patterns in the opponent's choices. Stay steady in knockout rounds.

Who should play it

Suits fans of the earlier games and anyone who likes short competitive bursts. The presentation is more modern. It remains a penalties-only game, which limits variety.
