Accessibility

A site full of games should be easy for everyone to get around. Here is where Retro-Bowl-2027 stands today, without the marketing.

What works

  • Tab and Enter reach every link, filter and button, and the focused item is outlined.
  • Thumbnails carry the game's title as alt text, so a screen reader announces real names, not file names.
  • Pale text sits on dark panels, which keeps contrast high.
  • Pages scale down to a phone and up to a wide monitor. Browser zoom works without cutting anything off.
  • Every game has a written guide with its controls listed, so you know before loading whether a game needs a mouse, keys or touch.

What does not

The games come from outside studios and run in an embedded frame. Most were built for a mouse and keyboard and say little to a screen reader. We cannot rewrite them. The best we can do is state their requirements clearly in each guide.

Report a barrier

Stuck somewhere? Email contact@retro-bowl-2027.github.io with the page address, what you tried and the browser or assistive tool you used. Each report gets read, and fixes on our side are made as soon as possible.
