Kingdom Rush – Four tower types, heroes and a rain of fire

Ironhide's Kingdom Rush is often named among the best tower defense games ever made, and its browser release shows why. Enemies march along winding paths toward your kingdom, and you answer with four kinds of towers: archers, barracks, mages and artillery. Add heroes, special powers and smart upgrades, and every stage becomes a satisfying puzzle.

The basics of Kingdom Rush

Build towers on fixed spots along the road, then upgrade them as gold flows in. Archers hit fast, barracks send soldiers to block the road, mages cut through armor and artillery deals splash damage. Fully upgraded towers branch into specialized versions. Call down a rain of fire or drop reinforcements to handle emergencies. Stop the waves before too many enemies escape to win the stage.

Getting started

  • Place barracks at bends to hold enemies under tower fire.
  • Pair mages with armored enemies and artillery with crowds.
  • Use rain of fire on clusters, not single enemies.
  • Earn stars on early stages to buy upgrades for later ones.

Tips from experience

Barracks are the key to many stages. Soldiers hold enemies in place while archers and artillery work. Read the wave preview and adjust: fliers ignore barracks, and armored units shrug off arrows. Do not push one tower to the maximum early; balance coverage first. Call waves early only when your defense is comfortable, since it earns bonus gold.

Who should play it

Excellent for tower defense fans of any level. Difficulty settings and upgrades let you tune the challenge. Some later stages are tough on the hardest setting, which may test your patience.
