Cut the Rope – Swipe the ropes and feed the candy to Om Nom

A small green monster named Om Nom wants candy, and you hold the scissors. ZeptoLab's 2010 physics puzzle launched a long-running series with that premise. Candy dangles from ropes. Cut them at the right moment and the sweet swings, falls and lands in Om Nom's mouth. Cut wrong and it drops off the screen.

The basics of Cut the Rope

A swipe through a rope severs it. Because the candy carries momentum, letting it swing before you slice changes where it ends up. Tap the gadgets in a level to redirect the sweet: a bubble lifts it, an air cushion gives it a sideways puff. Three stars float along each route. Om Nom eating the candy ends the level; eating it after all three stars makes it perfect.

Controls

  • Drag – cut
  • Click – action

Getting started

  • Let the candy sway back and forth a couple of times before you slice.
  • Choose the first rope by asking which cut aims the candy at a star.
  • Tap a bubble only after the candy has risen to the height you need.
  • Go back to cleared levels for any stars you skipped.

Tips from experience

When you cut matters as much as what you cut. Slice at the peak of a swing to throw the candy far, or at the bottom for a short drop. Map the whole route first: the stars, the order of the ropes, the landing. Spikes and other hazards double as timing markers. Once found, most perfect solutions look obvious.

Who should play it

Great for all ages and for anyone who likes clever, bite-sized physics puzzles. Levels are short. The early boxes are easy, and the touch-first design means some solutions feel more natural on a phone.
