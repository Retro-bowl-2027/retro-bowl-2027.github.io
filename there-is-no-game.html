There Is No Game – The narrator insists there is nothing to play

A voice keeps telling you there is no game here. It complains, interrupts and tries to stop you from touching anything. There Is No Game, created by KaMiZoTo in June 2015, turns that stubborn narrator into the puzzle. Every solution comes from finding a way around his objections, often by poking at things he would rather you ignore.

The basics of There Is No Game

The screen looks almost empty, and the narrator insists you should leave. Click, drag and experiment with every element you can find. Letters, buttons and other bits of the interface often turn out to be movable or breakable. Each discovery moves the story forward and annoys the narrator a little more. Solve each scene to progress.

Getting started

  • Click on everything, including text and parts of the screen.
  • Drag objects to see whether they move or break.
  • Listen closely to the narrator's complaints, which often hide hints.
  • Try the obvious action he tells you not to do.

Tips from experience

Ignore the narrator and test everything. The puzzles hide in places you would not normally treat as interactive, such as titles and menus. Treat the whole interface as a toy. When stuck, revisit pieces you already touched; they may behave differently now. The jokes usually point toward the answer.

Who should play it

Great for players who enjoy clever, funny puzzle games that break the rules. It is short. Players who prefer straightforward goals may find the meta jokes confusing at first.
