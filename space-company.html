Space Company Online

Space Company – Grow a resource business into a spacefaring company

Start small, think big. Space Company is an HTML5 management and incremental game where a handful of basic resources slowly turns into a business that reaches into space. You gather, build machines to gather for you and research technology that opens the next stage. Efficiency is the skill. Waste slows everything down.

The basics of Space Company

Collect resources by hand at first, then build machines that produce them automatically. Machines often consume one resource to make another, so production chains need balance. Research unlocks better machines and new kinds of resources. Each new unlock widens the company's reach, eventually beyond the planet. Keep every chain running to grow steadily.

Getting started

  • Gather the first resources by hand to afford your earliest machines.
  • Build producers for the resources that everything else depends on.
  • Check which machines consume other resources before building many.
  • Put early research into technology that boosts basic output.

Tips from experience

Watch for bottlenecks. When one resource runs out, every machine that needs it stops. Grow supply before adding consumers. Read research descriptions to plan the next few unlocks. Check storage limits so production is not wasted. Return regularly to rebalance as new machines appear.

Who should play it

Ideal for incremental fans who enjoy planning production chains. Progress is slow and steady. Players who prefer fast feedback may find the early stages long.
