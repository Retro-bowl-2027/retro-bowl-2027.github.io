Toss the Turtle – Fire the turtle, then keep it flying

A cannon, a turtle and a very long field. Foreverkul's Toss the Turtle, a Flash launcher from August 2009, is about distance. Fire at a good angle, then keep the poor creature airborne with bullets and explosives. The ground is scattered with things that either fling the turtle onward or stop it flat. Money from each flight buys more firepower.

The basics of Toss the Turtle

Every flight begins at the cannon. Pick your moment to fire. In the air, shoot the turtle to nudge it higher and set off explosives for a bigger push. Down below, some objects bounce it along while others end the flight on the spot. Distance turns into cash, which buys a stronger cannon, better guns and more ammo for the next attempt.

Getting started

  • Fire the cannon when the barrel points high, but not straight up.
  • Save gun shots for when the turtle starts to drop.
  • Spend early money on cannon power and ammo.
  • Watch for ground objects that help the turtle bounce.

Tips from experience

Ammo is distance. Use shots sparingly early in a flight and save some for the end. Bombs give bigger boosts, so use them when the turtle is low. Upgrade evenly; a strong cannon needs guns to keep the turtle moving.

Who should play it

Great for fans of classic launcher games and steady upgrades. Each run is short. The loop repeats a lot, so it works best in short sessions.
