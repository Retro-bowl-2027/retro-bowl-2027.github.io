Cut the Rope Holiday – A festive box of candy-delivery puzzles

Om Nom's hunger does not take holidays. This seasonal edition from ZeptoLab wraps the classic rope-cutting puzzle in festive decorations and fresh levels, but the job is the same: get the candy to the little green monster. Holiday colors set the mood, while the physics stay as precise as ever.

The basics of Cut the Rope Holiday

Cut ropes by dragging across them, and click special objects to change the candy's path. The candy swings, falls and bounces according to physics, so the order and timing of each cut decide where it ends up. Stars hang along the way for players who want a perfect run. Deliver the candy into Om Nom's mouth to clear the level.

Controls

  • Drag – cut
  • Click – action

Getting started

  • Study each level's layout and stars before cutting anything.
  • Cut the ropes in an order that swings the candy toward the stars.
  • Click special objects at the moment the candy reaches them.
  • Restart quickly when the candy misses; levels are short.

Tips from experience

Every level is a short sequence of moves, so think in steps: first cut, second cut, object, landing. Let the candy settle before cutting when precision matters. Swinging builds momentum, and releasing at the high point sends it farthest. If a star seems out of reach, there is almost always a way, often through an object you have not used yet.

Who should play it

A nice seasonal pick for fans of the series and for families. It plays like the original. Players who have finished the main games will find a familiar challenge rather than a new one.
