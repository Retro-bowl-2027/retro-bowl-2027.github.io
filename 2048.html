2048 Online

2048 – Slide, merge, double, and reach the target

Sixteen cells and a handful of numbered tiles. That is the whole board in Gabriele Cirulli's 2014 puzzle, and it has kept people sliding ever since. Push every tile in one direction, combine matching pairs into double their value, and keep building until one tile reads 2048.

The basics of 2048

Each move shifts all tiles as far as they go up, down, left or right. Two equal tiles that collide merge into one, so 8 and 8 make 16. Each move also drops a fresh 2 or 4 into an empty cell. Reach 2048 to win, or keep playing past it for a higher score. The game ends when the grid is full and no merge is possible.

Controls

  • Arrow / WASD Keys – move Number Tiles

Getting started

  • Choose a corner, usually bottom left, and keep your largest tile there.
  • Rely on two directions, for example down and left, for nearly every move.
  • Fill the row along your corner so the big tile cannot be pushed out.
  • Touch a third key only when neither main direction works.

Tips from experience

Build a snake. Order the tiles along your corner row from largest to smallest, then continue along the row above in the opposite direction. Each merge then feeds the next. Never move away from your corner unless forced, and when you must, slide back right away. Small tiles stranded between big ones end most runs, so deal with them early.

Who should play it

A clean brain teaser for a few minutes at a time. Anyone can learn it in one move. Once you know the corner method, the game gets more mechanical, and the luck of new tiles still decides some runs.
