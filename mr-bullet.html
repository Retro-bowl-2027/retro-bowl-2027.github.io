Mr Bullet Online

Mr Bullet – Count your bullets, bend the shot

Lion Studios released Mr Bullet in July 2019 around a neat idea: a secret agent with very few bullets and a room full of enemies. Every shot has to count. Bullets bounce off walls, set off barrels and knock heavy objects loose. The best solutions often take out several targets with one well placed shot.

The basics of Mr Bullet

Each level shows the agent, a set of enemies and the objects around them. Hold to aim, line up the shot, and release to fire. A bullet travels, ricochets and hits whatever it meets. Clear every enemy before your ammo runs out. Explosive barrels, glass and heavy objects let you reach enemies you cannot hit directly.

Controls

  • Hold – aim
  • Release – shoot

Getting started

  • Look at the whole level before aiming and find objects that can do damage.
  • Aim at explosive barrels first when enemies stand close to them.
  • Bank shots off walls to get around cover.
  • Restart and try a new angle when a level needs too many bullets.

Tips from experience

Think like a pool player. Plan the bullet's path after the first wall as carefully as the opening angle. One bullet that drops a heavy object or triggers a barrel saves ammo for later. Small aim changes make big differences after a bounce, so adjust slowly. When a shot misses, note how far off it was and correct from there.

Who should play it

Ideal for puzzle fans who enjoy angles and chain reactions. Levels are short and easy to replay. The cartoon violence is mild, but the theme is still shooting, which some parents may want to know.
