This Is The Only Level – Same room, new rules, every single time

John Cooney's This Is The Only Level, a Flash favorite from August 2009, runs on a running gag. You see one room with a few hazards and an exit pipe, and you never see another. Yet each clear of that room changes how it works. Familiar ground turns strange, and every repeat is a fresh puzzle in disguise.

The basics of This Is The Only Level

Steer the little blue elephant across the room to the pipe on the far side. The layout never moves, but the game tweaks something with every stage, from how the keys respond to how the world behaves. A brief caption at the start of each stage hints at what has changed. Work out the twist, reach the pipe and the next variation begins.

Getting started

  • Memorize the room on the first, normal stage; you will see it many times.
  • Read each stage's caption closely before pressing anything.
  • Tap each key early to discover which controls have changed.
  • Try odd ideas when ordinary movement stops working.

Tips from experience

The caption is the key. Almost every stage makes sense once you decode its title. Think sideways, because the answer may not involve walking at all. The hazards stay in the same spots, so learn them once and they stop being a threat. Patience cracks even the strangest stages.

Who should play it

Ideal for puzzle platformer fans who enjoy clever twists. It is a Flash classic with a strong reputation. A few stages are cryptic, which may frustrate players who dislike guessing.
