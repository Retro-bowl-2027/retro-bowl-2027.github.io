Fleeing the Complex Online

Fleeing the Complex – Bust Henry out with one bad idea after another

Henry Stickmin is locked inside a high-security facility called the Wall, and getting out takes every ridiculous plan you can think of. PuffballsUnited's 2015 entry is one of the best-loved in the series, known for sharp jokes, smooth animation and a huge tree of possible outcomes. Most choices fail in spectacular ways.

The basics of Fleeing the Complex

At each decision point, several options appear on screen, and you click one to see what Henry does. Successful choices push the escape forward along one of several routes. Failures play a short, funny scene and send you back to try again. Different paths lead to different endings, and some bring in new characters who change the story.

Controls

  • Click – action

Getting started

  • Pick any option first and enjoy the result, failure or not.
  • Return to each choice and try the remaining options.
  • Keep track of which route you are on, since paths branch early.
  • Replay after one ending to find the others.

Tips from experience

Think like a collector. Each fail is a joke worth seeing, so clear every option before moving on. Some choices come up quickly, so read them fast. Pay attention to references to earlier Henry games, which add to the humor. Finding every ending is the real completion goal, and it takes several playthroughs.

Who should play it

A highlight of the series and a great entry point for newcomers who like comedy. It runs longer than earlier Henry games. There is little challenge, so it is more interactive cartoon than puzzle.
