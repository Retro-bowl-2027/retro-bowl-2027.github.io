Gravity Soccer – Soccer where every bounce follows the physics

Gravity Soccer plays by the rules of physics more than the rules of football. Qky Game Studio's HTML5 game gives the ball and the players weight and momentum, so shots arc, rebound and roll in ways a normal football game would smooth away. Learning how the ball moves is the first step to scoring with it.

The basics of Gravity Soccer

Move your player, time your kicks and try to put the ball into the goal. Because physics governs everything, the angle and moment of contact decide where the ball goes. Bounces off players and surfaces can help or hurt. Reading those bounces lets you set up shots and spoil your opponent's. Score more than the other side to win.

Getting started

  • Kick the ball a few times to see how it arcs.
  • Approach the ball at an angle to aim your kicks.
  • Defend by keeping your player on the goal side of the ball.
  • Use bounces off surfaces to reach awkward angles.

Tips from experience

Read the bounce. Watch how the ball comes off each surface and position yourself where it will land, not where it is. Short, controlled kicks are easier to predict than big hits. Use the ball's momentum: striking it while it already rolls toward the goal adds to its speed. Patience on defense often creates counterattacks.

Who should play it

Fits players who enjoy physics-driven sports games and unusual takes on football. Matches are easy to start. The physics can feel unpredictable, which may frustrate anyone wanting precise control.
