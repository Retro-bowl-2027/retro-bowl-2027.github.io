Biker Street – Balance down a steampunk street for distance

Steampunk props, steep hills and jagged rocks line the road. IriySoft set this 2017 bike game on a strange Victorian-style street and asks one thing of you: ride as far as you can before something goes wrong. Collect coins on the way, push your distance and protect your high score.

The basics of Biker Street

Up moves you forward and Down rolls you back, while Left and Right tilt the rider to shift weight. Hills are steep, and rocks and drops can flip the bike if you hit them badly. Your run lasts until you crash. Distance and coins both feed your score, so you weigh speed against safety the whole way.

Controls

  • Up Arrow – forward
  • Down Arrow – backward
  • Left / Right Arrow – tilt

Getting started

  • Ride slowly over the first hills to learn how the bike tilts.
  • Lean forward on climbs so the front wheel stays down.
  • Ease off before sharp rocks rather than hitting them at speed.
  • Grab coins only when they lie on a safe line.

Tips from experience

Weight shifts keep you alive. Tilt forward going uphill and back on steep descents, in short taps rather than long holds. Speed helps you clear gaps but hurts you on rough ground, so read the road ahead and slow down early. Reverse can rescue a bad approach if you catch it in time. Treat coins as a bonus, never as a reason to take a risk.

Who should play it

Suits fans of physics bike games and distance challenges. The odd setting gives it character. The core loop is the same every run, so it lasts as long as chasing your own record does.
