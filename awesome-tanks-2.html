Awesome Tanks 2 Online

Awesome Tanks 2 – Blast through the levels, then upgrade the tank

Drive, aim, fire, upgrade. Alexander Gettle's top-down shooter from 2012 runs on that loop, and it works. You command a single tank against levels full of enemy vehicles. Every victory pays out money, and the shop turns that money into stronger guns, better armor and new weapons for the next fight.

The basics of Awesome Tanks 2

WASD or the arrows drive the tank, the cursor points the turret, and a click fires. Number keys swap between the weapons you own. A level is won by wiping out every hostile unit while staying alive. Between levels you spend your earnings on upgrades and new guns. Tougher enemies appear as you advance, so a tank that skips upgrades soon falls behind.

Controls

  • Arrow / WASD Keys – move
  • Click – shoot
  • Mouse – aim
  • Number Keys – switch Weapons

Getting started

  • Spend your first earnings on armor or health before bigger guns.
  • Duck behind walls while your turret keeps shooting.
  • Back away from groups so you fight enemies one or two at a time.
  • Switch weapons with the number keys to match the enemy type.

Tips from experience

Split the enemy up. Pull one or two vehicles toward you by peeking around a wall, then retreat behind it while they follow. Keep moving while you fire, because a stationary tank gets hit by everything. Balance your upgrades: a big gun on a fragile tank dies fast. Save money for key purchases instead of spreading it thin.

Who should play it

Ideal for fans of arcade shooters with a progression system. The upgrade loop makes each level feel like a step forward. It needs mouse aim and a keyboard, so it does not suit touch screens.

Game info
