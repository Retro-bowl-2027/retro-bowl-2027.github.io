n-gon – Physics, polygons and upgrades that change everything

Simple polygons make up every enemy, block and wall in n-gon, a physics-heavy platform shooter by quadape. The look is plain, yet objects react with real weight and momentum. You move through levels, fight what attacks you and pick up upgrades that reshape how you play. Each run can feel different from the last.

The basics of n-gon

Each level is a space of platforms, loose objects and hostile shapes. Move, jump and shoot your way to the exit. Objects in the environment can be pushed, knocked around and used to your advantage. Along the way, upgrades change your weapons, your defenses or your movement. Combining them well is the key to surviving deeper levels.

Getting started

  • Clear the first level carefully to learn how objects react to hits.
  • Read each upgrade's description before picking it, since some change how you move.
  • Pick upgrades that work together instead of grabbing whatever appears.
  • Keep moving during fights so enemies cannot crowd you.

Tips from experience

Use the physics. Heavy blocks can shield you, hit enemies or block a doorway. Fight from above when possible, since you see more of the room. Build around one strong idea, such as a weapon type, and pick upgrades that support it. Do not rush; entering a room slowly lets you deal with enemies one group at a time.

Who should play it

Fits players who enjoy physics sandboxes and run-based upgrade systems. The plain look hides a lot of depth. Newcomers may find the many upgrade options confusing at first, so reading descriptions helps.
