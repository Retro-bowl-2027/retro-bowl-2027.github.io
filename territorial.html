Territorial – Spread your borders and swallow your neighbors

Released by David Tschacher in August 2020, Territorial is an empire strategy game where a tiny patch of land grows into a sprawling nation, or gets swallowed by one. Your land produces troops. Those troops expand into empty ground or attack your neighbors. Grow too slowly and you fall behind. Attack too greedily and you leave yourself exposed.

The basics of Territorial

You start with a small territory on a map shared with rivals. Your army grows over time, and you choose how much of it to send when expanding or attacking. Empty land is easy to claim; enemy land costs troops to take. Keep enough at home to defend. The last nation standing, or the largest, comes out on top.

Getting started

  • Expand into empty land quickly in the opening minutes.
  • Send only part of your army so your borders stay defended.
  • Pick on weaker neighbors before you take on strong ones.
  • Watch rival sizes so nobody grows large without you noticing.

Tips from experience

Timing is everything. Early expansion builds your economy, but overreaching invites an attack. Hit a neighbor while it is busy fighting someone else. Avoid opening too many fronts at once. In the late game, patience often beats aggression, and a well-defended core outlasts a sprawling but thin empire.

Who should play it

Great for strategy fans who like fast, competitive matches. Rounds are short and intense. The fast pace can overwhelm newcomers, and some matches are decided quickly by early mistakes.
