Sandboxels – Paint with matter and let the reactions run

Pick a material, paint it onto the screen and see what it does to its neighbors. Sandboxels is a falling-pixel simulation with a huge menu of substances, from plain dirt to molten rock, each obeying its own rules. Nothing needs winning. The fun lies in setting up a mix and watching the chain reaction you did not expect.

The basics of Sandboxels

Choose a substance from the category tabs and brush it onto the canvas. Grains pile into heaps, liquids pool and run downhill, vapors drift upward, and many materials ignite, freeze or dissolve under the right conditions. When two substances meet, they may produce something new entirely. Build, wreck and rebuild as often as you like. Every session is an open experiment.

Getting started

  • Begin with a few basic substances to see how grains and liquids behave.
  • Pair two materials side by side and watch what forms where they touch.
  • Open each category tab to find stranger substances to test.
  • Wipe the canvas and start over whenever it gets too crowded.

Tips from experience

Treat it like a lab. Change one variable at a time so you know which ingredient caused the result. Draw a container before pouring anything runny. Start with small doses, since large reactions can swamp the whole screen. Write down the combinations worth repeating.

Who should play it

Great for curious players of any age who like tinkering. Nothing can be lost or failed. Anyone who needs objectives or a score will likely drift away without a goal to chase.
