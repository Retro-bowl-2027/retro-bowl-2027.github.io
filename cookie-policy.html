Cookie Policy

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Similar tools, such as local storage, keep data on your device in the same way. This page explains which ones Retro-Bowl-2027 uses and how to control them.

Analytics cookies

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which games and guides people use. Its cookies record things like repeat visits and how long you stayed. The data reaches us in aggregate form. We use it to fix problems and decide which guides to improve.

Advertising cookies

We show ads through Google AdSense. Google and other ad vendors use cookies to choose which ads to show, limit how often you see the same ad, and measure ad performance. Some of these cookies let ads be based on your visits to this site and other sites. You can turn off personalized ads at adssettings.google.com or opt out of some vendors at www.aboutads.info/choices.

Visitors from the EEA, the UK and Switzerland see a consent message first. Advertising and analytics cookies are only set according to the choice you make there.

Storage set by games

The games are made and hosted by third parties. Many of them save progress, high scores and settings in cookies, local storage or IndexedDB. This storage belongs to the game provider. Clearing it resets your progress in that game.

How to manage cookies

  • Open your browser's privacy or site settings to view, block or delete cookies.
  • Block third-party cookies to limit ad tracking across sites.
  • Use a private window if you do not want cookies kept after you close it.
  • Install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on to stop analytics collection.

Blocking all cookies can stop some games from saving or loading. If a game misbehaves, allow cookies for the game's provider and try again.
