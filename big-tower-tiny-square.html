Big Tower Tiny Square – Climb a giant tower one deadly room at a time

A very small square faces a very tall tower. Evil Objective's 2018 precision platformer turns that size gap into a long climb through rooms lined with spikes and moving traps. Each room is short. Few are easy. Deaths are instant and retries are quick, so the challenge comes from mastering one tricky screen after another.

The basics of Big Tower Tiny Square

You run, jump and wall jump your way upward. Every room is a small obstacle course with a clear pattern to learn. Touch a hazard and you restart from the last checkpoint, usually close by. Moving platforms and timed hazards demand exact inputs. Progress means reaching the top, one solved room after another, without losing patience along the way.

Getting started

  • Practice wall jumps on the first walls until the timing feels natural.
  • Watch a room's hazards for one full cycle before you move.
  • Break long rooms into short sections and master each one.
  • Take a breather after a string of deaths instead of rushing.

Tips from experience

Precision platformers reward calm hands. Short, controlled taps give finer movement than holding a direction. Learn where each moving hazard will be, not where it is, and time your jump to that. Many rooms have a safe spot to pause; find it. When a room beats you repeatedly, change one thing about your approach, such as jump timing, and test it.

Who should play it

Built for fans of tough platformers like Super Meat Boy who enjoy repetition and mastery. Rooms are short, so failure stings less. Players who dislike dying often should look elsewhere.
