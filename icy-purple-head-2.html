Icy Purple Head 2 – Switch between ice and rubber to reach the goal

A purple head with two states is the whole toolkit in Mini Duck Games' physics puzzle. Hold the button and it freezes into a slick block of ice. Let go and it turns soft and bouncy again. Every level asks you to switch between the two at the right moments to steer the head to its goal.

The basics of Icy Purple Head 2

Pressing and holding freezes the head; letting go thaws it. Frozen, it glides over surfaces and can stay put in places where a bouncy head would tumble off. Thawed, it rebounds and grips. Levels combine slopes, gaps and obstacles that need both states in sequence. Reach the goal to clear the level.

Controls

  • Hold – turns Icy
  • Release – turns Normal

Getting started

  • Try each state on flat ground to feel the difference.
  • Freeze before slopes you want to slide down quickly.
  • Thaw when you need to stop or bounce upward.
  • Plan the switch points along the route before starting.

Tips from experience

Think of the two forms as tools. Ice is for speed and sliding along surfaces; the normal form is for stopping, bouncing and grip. Most levels need a switch at an exact moment, so watch closely where things go wrong and adjust by a fraction. Early levels teach the rules and later ones combine them, so remember each trick.

Who should play it

Good for players who enjoy short physics puzzles with a clever mechanic. Levels are quick. The difficulty stays moderate, so seasoned puzzle fans may finish it fast.
