Trimps Online

Trimps – Gather, breed, fight and grow without end

Greensatellite released Trimps in May 2015, and it starts deceptively small. You gather food, wood and metal for a few small creatures called trimps. Before long you are researching upgrades, building housing, sending armies into battle and managing a world far larger than it first appears. Few incremental games grow this deep.

The basics of Trimps

Collect resources by hand, then put trimps to work gathering for you. Build housing so the population can grow. Research upgrades that make every job more efficient. Send trimps into battle through a series of zones to earn rewards and unlock new features. Each stage introduces another system, and progress continues while you are away from the screen.

Getting started

  • Gather food, wood and metal by hand to start.
  • Build housing early so the population can grow.
  • Assign trimps to jobs that keep every resource flowing.
  • Read each new unlock to understand what it adds.

Tips from experience

Balance is key. A large army needs a strong economy behind it. Do not rush into battles your army cannot win; strengthen production first. Read each new feature as it unlocks, since many change the best strategy. Check back regularly, because several systems build up over time and need attention.

Who should play it

Great for incremental fans who enjoy deep, layered systems. It can last a very long time. New players may find the early pace slow before the game opens up.
