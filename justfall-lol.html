JustFall.lol – Stay on the platforms longer than everyone else

The ground is vanishing and so are your rivals. JustPlay.LOL's 2021 party game crams a lobby of players onto stages that crack, shift and fall away beneath them. Sprint, hop and lunge between safe patches while the others drop into the abyss. Whoever survives longest takes the round.

The basics of JustFall.lol

Every round brings a stage with its own trick. On some, tiles break wherever someone stands; on others, the floor slides or tips. Your job is to keep your feet on something solid for as long as possible, using runs, jumps and lunges. Fall and you are out. Rounds move fast, and the group thins until only a few remain.

Getting started

  • Watch how the floor breaks in the first seconds of each stage.
  • Keep moving on stages where tiles break underfoot.
  • Jump to safe areas early rather than at the last moment.
  • Stay away from crowds, where tiles disappear fastest.

Tips from experience

Space is your friend. Crowds destroy platforms quickly, so move to quieter areas. On tile stages, plan a path that leaves solid ground behind you for later. Use jumps to cross gaps, but avoid unnecessary ones; landing badly is a common way out. Patience often beats speed in the final moments.

Who should play it

Great for players who enjoy Fall Guys style party survival. Rounds are short and lively. Results depend partly on other players and luck, so skill does not always win.
