Cookie Clicker – Bake one cookie, then billions

A big cookie sits on the left of the screen. Click it and you get one cookie. Orteil's 2013 browser game, one of the titles that defined the idle genre, takes that humble start and spirals it into a cookie empire of grandmas, farms, factories and stranger things.

The basics of Cookie Clicker

Each click on the giant cookie adds one to your stock, and that stock buys buildings that bake around the clock, every tier dearer and more productive than the one before. Upgrades multiply what clicks and buildings produce. Every so often a golden cookie drifts onto the screen, and catching it triggers a brief, enormous bonus. Far down the line, ascending wipes the bakery for prestige that speeds up every later run.

Getting started

  • Tap the giant cookie until a first cursor or grandma is affordable.
  • Keep adding buildings, and snap up upgrades the moment they unlock.
  • Catch each golden cookie that floats by; its bonus dwarfs normal output.
  • Keep the tab open in the background so the ovens never stop.

Tips from experience

Judge purchases by payback time, not sticker price. A pricier building that bakes far more can earn its cost back sooner than a cheap one. Upgrades are almost always the best buy when they appear. Combine boosts: a golden cookie multiplier plus fast clicking can bank hours of production in seconds. For a long game, export your save from the options menu, because clearing browser data erases the bakery.

Who should play it

A classic for anyone curious about idle games, and still one of the best. It rewards checking in over days. The appeal is pure number growth, so players who want goals or action will lose interest fast.
