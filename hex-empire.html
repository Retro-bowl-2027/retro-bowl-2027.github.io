Hex Empire – Grow your hex kingdom and capture every capital

Hexagon tiles, a handful of competing empires and armies that swell as your borders spread. Minijuegos' Hex Empire is turn-based conquest boiled down to its essentials. March troops across the map, take towns and press into hostile territory until no rival capital is left standing. The other empires want the same thing, so when you strike matters.

The basics of Hex Empire

Every turn comes with a small budget of moves. Spend them pushing armies forward, claiming empty or hostile hexes and taking towns that feed your strength. Two armies can merge into one bigger force. Attack a smaller army and you gain ground; attack a larger one and you lose troops. Each hex touches six others, so an exposed flank is easy to hit. Capture every capital to win.

Getting started

  • Seize the neutral towns closest to you in the first turns.
  • Merge small armies before you strike at a strong one.
  • Leave at least one solid force guarding your capital.
  • Hit rivals where their lines are thin, not where they are massed.

Tips from experience

Grab land quickly, then dig in. Early turns are a race for undefended towns. Later, pour your moves into a single front instead of scattering them. When two rivals clash, let them weaken each other and then pounce on the survivor. With so few moves per turn, never waste one marching an army back and forth.

Who should play it

A strong pick for anyone after short, turn-based conquest with clear rules. A full game does not take long. Computer rivals can be predictable, and fans of heavier strategy may want more depth.
