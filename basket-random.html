Basket Random Online

Basket Random – One button, floppy arms, random courts

Two players, one key each, and physics that refuse to cooperate. RHM Interactive's 2020 basketball game makes clumsiness the main feature. Players wobble on rubbery legs, the ball pings around unpredictably and the court changes every round. Scoring feels half earned and half lucky, which is exactly the point.

The basics of Basket Random

Each side has a single button that makes its players jump and swing their arms. Timing that press decides whether you grab the ball, block a shot or flail into the air. Get the ball into the opponent's hoop to score. After each point the setup can change, bringing new court conditions to deal with. First to the target score wins the match.

Getting started

  • Press your key once to see how your players jump and swing.
  • Time presses to reach the ball at the top of its bounce.
  • Tap rather than hold when your players are already off balance.
  • Watch how each new court changes movement before committing.

Tips from experience

Rhythm beats spam. Pressing constantly sends your players tumbling, so wait for the ball to come near and then press with purpose. Arm swings carry the ball, so jump just as it reaches you to push it toward the hoop. Stay near your own basket when the opponent has control. Shrug off the flukes; they go both ways.

Who should play it

Great for two people sharing one keyboard and for quick laughs. Rounds last seconds. Players who want real basketball tactics or consistent control will find the randomness more annoying than funny.
