Slime Rush TD – Build towers, stop the slime waves

Waves of slimes march across the map toward the end of the path. Slime Rush TD is an HTML5 tower defense where your towers must stop them before they get through. Slimes come in different sizes and speeds. A good mix of towers and smart early placement decide whether you hold the line.

The basics of Slime Rush TD

Place towers beside the path. Each tower attacks slimes in range. Earn money by defeating slimes and spend it on new towers or upgrades. Waves get bigger and tougher, with faster or larger slimes that need different answers. Stop every wave to win the level. Slimes that slip past cost you.

Getting started

  • Place your first towers near a bend in the path.
  • Mix tower types to handle both fast and large slimes.
  • Upgrade key towers instead of spreading money too thin.
  • Watch which slimes slip through and adjust your defense.

Tips from experience

Placement beats quantity. A tower near a curve hits slimes for longer. Upgrade towers that cover the most path. Save some money for emergencies in later waves. Build early, since towers placed late have less time to work. Cover the first stretch of path well.

Who should play it

Suits tower defense fans who like quick, colorful levels. The rules are easy to learn. Experienced strategy players may find it light compared with larger defense games.
