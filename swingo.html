Swingo – Attach, swing, let go, repeat

Blumgi's Swingo makes swinging the entire game. Attach to a point, swing and let go to fly toward the next point. A good release sends you far. A bad one wastes your momentum and drops you short. Reading the swing and planning the next attachment is where skill shows.

The basics of Swingo

Each level is a series of attachment points and hazards. Grab a point, swing to build speed and release at the right moment to launch. Catch the next point before you fall. Reach the goal to finish the level. Later levels add tighter gaps and trickier layouts that need cleaner releases.

Getting started

  • Swing a few times on the first point to feel the motion.
  • Release on the upswing, before the peak, for the best distance.
  • Look at the next point before letting go.
  • Restart quickly whenever a release goes wrong and adjust slightly.

Tips from experience

Momentum is precious. Build it before long gaps and keep it through each release. Small timing changes make big differences, so adjust slowly. Plan two points ahead on complex levels. Practice makes the timing automatic, and soon the swings feel natural.

Who should play it

Good for players who enjoy physics games and swinging mechanics. Levels are short and replayable. The timing can take practice, which may frustrate impatient players.
