X Trial Racing – Ride the rough ground without tipping over

Rocks, ramps and uneven ground stand between you and the finish line. X Trial Racing is an HTML5 trial-style racing game where balance matters more than speed. Accelerate, brake and lean to stay upright over every bump. The terrain gets tougher as you go, and careful riders get further.

The basics of X Trial Racing

Each level is a stretch of rugged ground between a start and a finish line. Use the throttle and brake to control your speed, and shift your weight to keep the bike level over rocks, ramps and slopes. A crash sends you back to the start of the level. New levels add steeper climbs, bigger drops and obstacles placed to throw you off balance.

Getting started

  • Ride the first level slowly to feel the bike's balance.
  • Lean forward on climbs and back on descents.
  • Ease off before big bumps rather than hitting them at full speed.
  • Restart quickly after a crash and adjust your approach.

Tips from experience

Smooth beats fast. Steady throttle keeps the bike stable, while sudden bursts lift the front wheel. Shift your weight early rather than reacting once the bike is already tipping. Look ahead to the next obstacle and set your speed in advance. When one section keeps beating you, try it slower first.

Who should play it

Suits fans of trial-style physics racing. Levels are short and easy to retry. Players wanting high-speed racing or rivals on the track may find the careful pace slow.
