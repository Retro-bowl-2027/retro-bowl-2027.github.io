Contact Retro-Bowl-2027

The fastest way to reach us is email: contact@retro-bowl-2027.github.io. There is no contact form and no phone line. Email keeps every request in writing, which makes it easier to track and answer.

What to include

A clear message gets a faster answer. Put these in your email:

  • A subject line that names the request, such as 'Broken game' or 'Guide correction'.
  • The full address of the page you are writing about.
  • What you expected and what happened instead.
  • Your browser and device, if you are reporting a technical problem.
  • A screenshot, if it helps show the issue.

Kinds of requests we handle

Broken or slow games

Tell us which game fails to load, freezes or shows an error. We test it and fix the page or remove the game.

Guide corrections

If a guide gets a control, rule or fact wrong, let us know what the right information is. We check it against the game and update the guide.

Game suggestions

Send the name of a free browser game you want added. We cannot promise to add every suggestion, but we review them all.

Copyright and removal requests

Game owners and rights holders can ask for a change or removal. For formal notices, follow the steps on our DMCA page.

Privacy questions

Ask about the data collected on retro-bowl-2027.github.io or make a privacy request. Our Privacy Policy explains what we collect and why.

Response time

We aim to reply within a few working days. Copyright notices and privacy requests are handled first.
