Super Mario Wonder – Colorful levels, stomps and hidden surprises

Bright stages, enemies to squash and secrets tucked into corners. This Super Mario Wonder is an unofficial browser game modeled on a recent Nintendo side-scroller, listed with an October 2023 date. The job is the classic one: get to the goal. Mastering how the hero moves, and sniffing out what each stage hides, makes that job easier. Nintendo owns the name and characters.

The basics of Super Mario Wonder

Make your way across side-scrolling stages full of foes, pits and pickups. Land on enemies from above to flatten them, steer clear of hazards and touch the goal to finish. Secret pickups reward careful searching. Stages are colorful and varied, and a good grip on running and jumping lets you clear them faster.

Getting started

  • Learn the run and jump controls on the opening level.
  • Stomp enemies from above instead of running into them.
  • Look for hidden items in odd corners and above the screen.
  • Replay levels to find what you missed the first time.

Tips from experience

Momentum helps. Running jumps cover more ground than standing ones. Watch enemy patterns before approaching. Search high and low; secrets often hide where the path seems to end. Take your time on a new stage, then replay it faster once you know where everything is.

Who should play it

Suits platformer fans who want something inspired by a recent Mario game. It is unofficial, so content and performance may differ from the original. It works best as a casual distraction.
