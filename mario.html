Mario Online

Mario – Run, jump and stomp through classic side-scrolling levels

Pipes, bricks, coins and a mustached hero with a famous jump. This unofficial browser game is inspired by Nintendo's side-scrolling classics, and the catalog lists 1985, the year the original arrived. Run right, stomp on enemies and reach the end of each colorful level. All characters and names belong to their owners.

The basics of Mario

Move right through each level, jumping over gaps, onto platforms and on top of enemies to defeat them. Blocks may hide coins or power-ups. Touching an enemy from the side costs you, while landing on top usually removes it. Reach the end of the stage to progress. As an unofficial version, details may differ from the original games.

Getting started

  • Run and jump through the first level to learn the jump height.
  • Hit blocks from below to see what they hide.
  • Stomp enemies by landing on top of them.
  • Watch for gaps and time your jumps before you reach the edge.

Tips from experience

Momentum helps. A running jump carries you much farther than a standing one, so build speed before big gaps. Deal with enemies one at a time and avoid jumping into groups. Explore for hidden blocks and items. If a section keeps beating you, slow down and watch enemy movement before committing.

Who should play it

Fits anyone nostalgic for classic platformers who wants a quick browser version. It is easy to start. Being unofficial, its feel and content may not match the real Nintendo games.
