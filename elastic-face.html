Elastic Face – Pull a rubbery face into silly shapes

David Li's 2021 WebGL toy puts a face on screen and lets you grab it. Pull a cheek, stretch a nose, drag the chin halfway down the page. The skin acts like stretchy rubber, wobbling as it moves and springing back once you let go. There is nothing to win. The only goal is making something funny.

The basics of Elastic Face

Click and hold anywhere on the face, then drag to stretch it. The harder you pull, the further it deforms. Release and it jiggles back into shape. You can grab different parts in turn to see how each one reacts. The physics are smooth, which is exactly what makes the toy satisfying to play with.

Getting started

  • Grab the nose first and pull it straight out.
  • Try pulling two different spots one after another.
  • Let go quickly after a long pull to see the biggest bounce.
  • Pull slowly for a smoother, more controlled stretch.

Tips from experience

Play with speed and direction. A fast pull and release creates a big wobble, while a slow drag gives a smooth stretch. Try grabbing small features for odd results. Combine pulls in quick succession before the face settles. It is a toy, so the best tip is simply to experiment.

Who should play it

Great for a minute of silliness and for showing friends. It needs only a mouse or a touch screen. There is no game here, so anyone expecting goals will lose interest immediately.
