Tank Trouble 2 Online

Tank Trouble 2 – Ricochet shells through the maze, and duck

Subterranean Software released Tank Trouble 2 in May 2015, and its idea is wonderfully mean. Tanks roam a top-down maze, and every shell bounces off the walls. That means you can hit an enemy around a corner. It also means your own shot can come straight back at you. Power-ups add stranger weapons to the chaos.

The basics of Tank Trouble 2

Drive your tank through the maze, aim and fire. Shells rebound off walls several times before fading, so every shot sets up a dangerous path for everyone, including you. Destroy the other tanks to win the round. Power-ups appear in the maze and give temporary special weapons. Rounds are short, and matches build up over many of them.

Getting started

  • Fire a few test shots to see how shells bounce in tight corridors.
  • Back away after shooting so your own shell does not hit you.
  • Grab power-ups when the path to them is safe.
  • Use bank shots to hit tanks hiding around corners.

Tips from experience

Think in angles. A shot at a wall can reach a tank you cannot see. Never fire straight at a nearby wall; the rebound will find you first. Keep moving after each shot. When an opponent grabs a power-up, retreat until you know what it does.

Who should play it

Ideal for friends sharing a keyboard and anyone who enjoys tactical chaos. Matches are fast and funny. Solo play is less lively, since the fun comes from outsmarting other people.
