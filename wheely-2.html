Wheely 2 – Help the little red car find its way

A small red car with big eyes and a lot of personality needs your help to get where it is going. Wheely 2 is an early chapter in a friendly point-and-click puzzle series. Each scene holds obstacles that stop the car in its tracks. Poke around, find the trick and clear the road.

The basics of Wheely 2

Every scene is a small puzzle between Wheely and the exit. Click on objects to see what they do, pick up useful items and use them where they help. Moving parts, switches and hidden helpers change the path. Get Wheely safely to the end of the scene to move on to the next one.

Getting started

  • Click on every object in the scene to see how it reacts.
  • Watch what happens when Wheely starts moving before you plan.
  • Pick up any item you can and look for where it fits.
  • Restart the scene if Wheely gets stuck or hurt.

Tips from experience

Look before you click. Many puzzles have a simple trick once you spot the right object. Watch the scene carefully for small details that move or glow. Try items on different parts of the scene. When a plan fails, retry with a different order.

Who should play it

Great for younger players and anyone who likes gentle puzzles. The art is bright and cheerful. Experienced puzzle fans may find it easy and short.
