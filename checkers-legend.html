Checkers Legend – Classic checkers against the computer

Twelve pieces each, a checkered board and a computer opponent waiting for your first move. InLogic Games' version of checkers sticks to the familiar rules. Move diagonally, jump to capture, and crown pieces that reach the far side. Take every enemy piece or leave your rival without a legal move to win.

The basics of Checkers Legend

Pieces step one square diagonally forward onto dark squares. When an enemy piece sits diagonally next to yours with an empty square beyond, you can jump it and remove it from the board; if a capture is available, you are required to take it. Several jumps can chain together in one turn. A piece that reaches the last row becomes a king and can move backward too.

Getting started

  • Aim to control the center squares early in the game.
  • Keep your back row filled to block enemy kings for as long as you can.
  • Check every turn for forced captures before you plan anything else.
  • Trade pieces when you are ahead to simplify the board.

Tips from experience

Forced captures are the heart of checkers strategy. You can offer a piece on purpose to drag an enemy into a position where you capture two or three in return. Look for those trades, and watch for the same trick against you. Pieces on the edge are safe but weak. Keep your formation tight and protect pieces close to crowning.

Who should play it

Ideal for anyone who knows checkers and wants a quick game against the computer. The rules are standard. Strong players may find the computer beatable once they learn its habits.
