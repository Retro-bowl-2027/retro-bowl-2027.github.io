Stack – Drop each slab right on top of the last

A slab slides across the top of your tower. You tap. It stops. Whatever hangs over the edge falls away, and the next slab starts sliding. Stack is a minimalist timing game where the tower only grows as wide as your accuracy allows. Perfect drops keep it full width. Sloppy ones shrink it fast.

The basics of Stack

Each new block moves back and forth above the stack. Tap or click to drop it. The part that overlaps the block below stays; the rest is trimmed off. The tower gets narrower with each miss, and the blocks move faster as it grows. When you miss the stack completely, the game ends. Height is your score.

Getting started

  • Watch two or three passes of the first block to learn its speed.
  • Tap when the block lines up exactly with the edge below.
  • Focus on one edge of the block rather than the whole shape.
  • Restart immediately and aim to beat your height.

Tips from experience

Rhythm beats reaction. Count the block's swing in your head and tap on the beat. Watch one edge only; it is easier to judge than the center. A perfect drop is worth more than a fast one. When the tower is thin, slow down and make every drop count.

Who should play it

Great for quick sessions and anyone who likes simple timing tests. One input makes it easy to start. The single mechanic does not change, so long play can feel repetitive.
