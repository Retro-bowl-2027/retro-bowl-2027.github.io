Super Mario Construct – A fan platformer in a familiar style

Familiar pipes, blocks and enemies, built by a fan. Super Mario Construct is an unofficial HTML5 platformer from LuigiBonus that borrows the look of a famous franchise. Run, jump and explore levels that echo classic side-scrollers. The mechanics feel familiar, so it is easy to pick up. It is not an official release.

The basics of Super Mario Construct

Move through side-scrolling levels, jumping on enemies, collecting items and avoiding hazards. Reach the end of each level to move on. The mechanics follow classic platformer rules: precise jumps, timing and exploration. Hidden areas and items reward players who look around. Names and characters belong to their owners, and this is a fan project.

Getting started

  • Learn the jump arc on the first level before chasing secrets.
  • Stomp enemies rather than avoiding every one of them.
  • Look for hidden blocks and pipes that lead to bonuses.
  • Replay finished levels to find the items you missed.

Tips from experience

Classic platformer habits apply. Jump from the very edge of platforms for maximum distance. Learn how enemies move before you approach them. Explore pipes and odd corners. Take your time on new levels, then speed up once you know the layout.

Who should play it

Good for fans of classic side-scrollers who want a familiar fan game. It is easy to start. As an unofficial project, content and polish may vary.
