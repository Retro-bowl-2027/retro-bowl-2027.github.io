Cartoon Mini Racing – Toy cars, tight corners and hidden shortcuts

Bright tracks and cars that look like toys set the tone for Brain Software's 2018 racer. Races are short and fast, corners are tight, and the tracks hide shortcuts for drivers who explore. Cross the line ahead of your rivals and learn each course until you can drive it from memory.

The basics of Cartoon Mini Racing

The arrow keys do all the driving. Rival cars race alongside you, and the first across the finish wins. A flipped or wedged car goes back on the road with H. Each circuit has its own layout and shortcuts that can save valuable seconds. Races are usually decided by who brakes later and who finds the quicker line.

Controls

  • Arrow Keys – drive
  • H – reset Car

Getting started

  • Drive the first race carefully to learn the track layout.
  • Lift off before hairpins and get back on the gas as the car straightens.
  • Look for shortcuts on each track and test them in later races.
  • Hit H the instant the car ends up on its roof or stuck in a wall.

Tips from experience

Corner speed wins races. Take tight turns wide on entry and tight at the apex to keep momentum. Shortcuts are only worth it if you can take them cleanly; a botched shortcut costs more than the normal route. Avoid bumping rivals in corners. Learn one track at a time until you drive it without thinking.

Who should play it

Good for younger players and anyone who likes cheerful arcade racing. Races are quick. Veteran racing fans may find it simple and the challenge mild.
