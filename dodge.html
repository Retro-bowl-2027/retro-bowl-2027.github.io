Dodge Online

Dodge – Move anywhere, avoid everything

Pure avoidance, nothing else. Dodge drops a single character into an open arena and sends objects drifting, sliding and darting toward it from every side. There is no weapon, no power-up to lean on and no level to finish. You stay untouched for as long as your eyes and hands allow, and the run lasts exactly that long.

The basics of Dodge

Your character can roam anywhere inside the play area. Hazards cross the screen on their own paths, and a single touch finishes the round. As seconds pass the arena fills up: extra objects join and the old ones pick up pace, so the open pockets get smaller. Since no spot stays quiet for long, steady drifting beats parking in one place.

Getting started

  • Open each round in the middle, with room on every side.
  • Glance at the borders, since that is where fresh hazards show up.
  • Slide in short, smooth strokes instead of wild sprints.
  • Always keep one clear lane behind you for a retreat.

Tips from experience

Use your peripheral vision. Staring at one hazard blinds you to the rest, so soften your focus and read the whole arena. Corners feel calm but leave you one exit. When things get crowded, shrink your movements and thread through gaps. The classic mistake is escaping one object straight into the next, so check where you are heading before you go.

Who should play it

Anyone can play it within seconds, which makes it a handy reflex check between other tasks. Rounds are brief. Variety is close to zero, so expect short sessions rather than long ones.
