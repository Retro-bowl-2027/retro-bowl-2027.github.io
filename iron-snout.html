Iron Snout Online

Iron Snout – One pig, two sides, endless wolves

A pig stands in the middle of the screen, and wolves come at him from both sides. SnoutUp Games' 2014 fighter is fast, funny and surprisingly deep. You punch, kick and dodge, and you can even send some attacks back the way they came. Every wave pushes the pace higher until something lands that you did not see coming.

The basics of Iron Snout

Wolves charge in from the left and right; some leap, others attack from a distance. You respond with directional strikes, jumps and ducks, mostly on the arrow keys. Each attack has a timing window. Strike too early and you whiff; too late and you get hit. Survive as long as you can and push your score.

Getting started

  • Strike toward whichever side a wolf is coming from.
  • Watch for high and low attacks and jump or duck to avoid them.
  • Wait until a wolf is close enough before striking.
  • Keep your eyes on both edges of the screen.

Tips from experience

Rhythm and reading. Wolves telegraph their attacks, so watch their movement as they approach and respond at the last safe moment. Do not panic-press; every miss leaves a gap. Projectiles can often be knocked back, so practice turning threats into attacks. When the screen gets busy, deal with the nearest danger first.

Who should play it

Great for players who enjoy quick, skill-based brawlers with silly humor. Rounds are short. It is cartoonishly violent, and the speed can overwhelm newcomers at first.
