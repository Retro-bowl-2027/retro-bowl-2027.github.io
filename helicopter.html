Helicopter – Hold to rise, release to fall, avoid the walls

One of the oldest ideas in browser gaming still works. A small helicopter flies through an endless cave, and the only control is up. Hold the mouse to rise, let go to sink. The cave walls close in, blocks hang in the way, and the run ends the instant you touch anything.

The basics of Helicopter

The helicopter moves forward automatically. Holding the button lifts it; releasing lets gravity pull it down. The cave's ceiling and floor shift constantly, and obstacles appear in the middle of the tunnel. Your score is the distance flown. Since one collision ends the run, smooth control matters more than quick reactions.

Controls

  • Click – fly Up

Getting started

  • Practice short holds to learn how fast the helicopter climbs.
  • Hover roughly in the center of the cave whenever you can.
  • Begin climbing or dropping early when the cave bends.
  • Pass obstacles with a gentle arc rather than a sudden jerk.

Tips from experience

Think in curves. The helicopter keeps momentum, so sudden holds or releases overshoot. Feather the button to draw smooth waves through the cave. Look ahead to where the tunnel will be in a second, not where it is now. When an obstacle appears, decide early whether to go over or under it, and commit.

Who should play it

A timeless reflex game that anyone can play within seconds. It runs on almost any device. It never changes, so it works best in short bursts.
