Parking Fury 3 Online

Parking Fury 3 – New places to park, trickier situations to solve

Released in October 2017 as the third entry, Parking Fury 3 takes the precision parking idea into new environments. The situations are more complex, with tighter angles and more obstacles between you and the space. You still have one job. Get into the marked spot without hitting a single thing along the way.

The basics of Parking Fury 3

Each level gives you a car, a set of obstacles and a marked spot. Drive there using forward, reverse and steering, then settle inside the lines. A single collision ends the attempt. New environments change the layout of each challenge, so routes that worked before will not always work here. Precision and patience decide every level.

Controls

  • Arrow / WASD Keys – move
  • P – pause

Getting started

  • Look at the whole layout before touching the keys.
  • Plan where you will turn and where you will need to reverse.
  • Keep your speed low through every narrow gap.
  • Line up the final approach so you enter the spot straight.

Tips from experience

Break the drive into stages and stop between them when needed. Watch the corners of your car rather than its center. In complex levels, backing into a space is sometimes easier than driving in forward. When a level fails often, try a different route instead of repeating the same one.

Who should play it

Suits series fans and players who enjoy slow, careful puzzles on wheels. The new environments keep it fresh. Like the earlier games, it can feel repetitive for players who prefer speed.
