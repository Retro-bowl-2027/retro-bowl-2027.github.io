Alien Hominid Online

Alien Hominid – One crashed alien against wave after wave of agents

The ship is wrecked, the pilot is a tiny yellow alien, and the men in suits are already on their way. The Behemoth's run-and-gun classic from the Flash era drops you straight into the chase. Blast through waves of agents, keep moving and survive a level of chaos that the bold cartoon art makes look harmless. It is short, hard and very fast.

The basics of Alien Hominid

You push from left to right, shooting agents who rush in from left and right and drop in from above. Enemies swarm quickly, and a single hit can cost a life, so dodging matters as much as firing. Jumping helps you avoid shots and get over attackers. Reach the end of each stretch, beat the bigger threats waiting there and keep your lives intact.

Getting started

  • Test each key on the first screen to learn your moves.
  • Keep firing in short bursts while you move forward.
  • Jump over groups of agents rather than standing your ground.
  • Learn each boss pattern before pushing for damage.

Tips from experience

Stay mobile. Standing still gets you surrounded within seconds. Move in short dashes and shoot toward the side with more agents. Watch for enemies who attack from a distance; they are harder to spot than the ones running at you. On boss fights, spend the first attempt only watching. Patterns repeat, and openings follow every big attack.

Who should play it

Made for fans of tough old-school action and early web games. The art and speed still hold up. It is unforgiving, and new players should expect many early defeats.
