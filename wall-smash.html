Wall Smash – Aim true and break the wall with fewer shots

A wall of bricks stands in your way, and you have a limited number of shots to bring it down. Wall Smash is an HTML5 physics game about choosing where to hit. The layout of the bricks decides how they fall. A smart shot can drop half the wall at once; a careless one wastes ammunition.

The basics of Wall Smash

Each level presents a wall and a set of shots. Aim, fire and watch the bricks react. Hitting a key brick can bring large sections down together, while hitting a sturdy area may barely dent it. Clear the wall before you run out of shots to finish the level. Later walls are arranged in trickier patterns.

Getting started

  • Look at how the bricks are stacked before firing your first shot.
  • Aim at bricks that support large sections above them.
  • Watch the full collapse before deciding on your next shot.
  • Retry a level with a new target when shots run low.

Tips from experience

Think like a demolition crew. Supporting bricks matter more than the ones on top. Low shots usually cause bigger collapses. Save at least one shot for cleanup at the end, because a few stubborn bricks often survive the big fall. Learn how different arrangements react.

Who should play it

Good for players who enjoy short physics puzzles with a satisfying crash. Levels are quick. The idea stays the same throughout, so it works best in short sessions.
