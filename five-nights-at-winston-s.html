Five Nights at winston's – A fan-made night shift with its own monsters

Calder Young's 2021 fan project lifts the survival structure of a well-known horror series and dresses it in a new setting with its own cast. You are stuck at a post through a string of nights, and something is working its way toward you. Familiar bones, unfamiliar faces.

The basics of Five Nights at winston's

Your position is fixed, so every bit of safety comes from the tools at hand and how you spend them. Danger builds slowly over the course of a night. Spot it, hear it, answer it, and do so without burning through limited supplies. Every night turns the pressure up. A shift usually collapses because one warning went unnoticed or one resource got spent too soon.

Getting started

  • Use night one to find out exactly what each tool does.
  • Keep the volume up; noises often announce trouble before you see it.
  • Spend scarce resources sparingly until you understand a night's tempo.
  • When a shift fails, name the threat that got you and adjust for it.

Tips from experience

Survival comes from a loop. Pick an order of checks and cycle through it at a steady pace, then bend the loop when a new danger shows up. Panic clicking drains what you need later. If the same moment keeps ending your night, spend the next attempt watching for the clue you overlooked.

Who should play it

Aimed at fans of night-shift horror who want a new spin from the fan community. Nights are short and tense. Jump scares are a big part of it, and they will not suit everyone.
