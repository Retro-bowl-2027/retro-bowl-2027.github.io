Achievement Unlocked – A hundred achievements, one small elephant

Most games hand out achievements as a side reward. John Cooney's 2008 Flash classic makes them the entire game. You steer a small elephant around a single screen, and almost anything you do, sensible or silly, might unlock one of one hundred achievements. The fun lies in working out what counts.

The basics of Achievement Unlocked

Move and jump with the arrow keys. The room holds platforms, objects and corners to explore, and your list of achievements fills in as you trigger them. Some unlock by accident within seconds. Others need a specific, unexpected action or a lot of repetition. There is no story and no level to beat. Your goal is a complete list of all one hundred.

Controls

  • Arrow Keys – move

Getting started

  • Move around the whole screen and touch everything once.
  • Jump on every platform and every ledge you can reach.
  • Open the achievement list now and then to track your progress.
  • Try the obvious silly ideas, since the game often rewards them.

Tips from experience

Think like a designer who likes jokes. Many achievements reward the strange or the tedious, such as repeating an action far more times than you normally would. Make a mental map of every object on screen and test each one from different sides. When progress stalls, repeat simple actions longer than feels reasonable. Patience unlocks more than skill does here.

Who should play it

Great for completionists and fans of game-design humor. It is short and needs only arrow keys. Once all one hundred are unlocked, there is no reason to return.
