Bicycle Stunt 3D Online

Bicycle Stunt 3D – Hit the ramp, hold the bike, stick the landing

Ramps, obstacles and big drops fill the courses in this 3D stunt biking game built in Unity. Speed gets you into the air, but only control brings you down in one piece. Every course asks for the same thing: approach each obstacle at the right pace, keep the bike level and land cleanly enough to carry on.

The basics of Bicycle Stunt 3D

You ride a bicycle through courses in a 3D world, building speed on the flat and launching off ramps. In the air, your control over the bike's angle decides how you come down. Touch down on both wheels and you ride on; land at a bad angle and you start over. Reach the end of the course to complete it and move on to the next.

Getting started

  • Ride the first ramp at moderate speed to learn the jump distance.
  • Line the bike up straight before every takeoff.
  • Level the bike in the air so both wheels touch down together.
  • Learn each course in sections and repeat the hardest jump until it sticks.

Tips from experience

Most crashes start before the jump. A crooked approach sends the bike off the ramp at an angle you cannot fix in the air, so straighten up early. Use less speed than you think; overshooting a landing is as bad as falling short. Make small corrections mid-air. If one obstacle keeps beating you, change your approach speed rather than your air control.

Who should play it

Suits players who enjoy physics stunts and learning courses by repetition. The 3D view makes jumps look good. It needs a reasonably capable computer for WebGL, and older machines may stutter.
