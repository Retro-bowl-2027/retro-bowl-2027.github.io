OvO Dimensions – Familiar moves, new rules to master

The core of OvO stays put in Dimensions: run, jump, slide, kick off walls. What changes are the levels. New mechanics reshape how stages work, so knowing the moves is only half the job. You also need to understand each new rule before you can use it well. Expect to think a little before you sprint.

The basics of OvO Dimensions

Each level is still a course from start to finish, but new mechanics change the path. Something that worked in earlier games may need a different approach here. Learn what each new element does, then combine it with sliding and wall jumping. Clear levels to move on, and replay them to find faster routes once you understand the tricks.

Controls

  • Arrow Keys – move

Getting started

  • Play every new mechanic slowly the first time you meet it.
  • Combine one new trick with your usual moves before chaining several.
  • Restart early when a run goes wrong instead of limping to the end.
  • Replay cleared levels to look for shorter, faster routes.

Tips from experience

Treat each new mechanic as a tool. Spend a few attempts just testing it, without caring about time. Then plan the route around it. Keep your old habits sharp; wall jumps and slides still carry most levels. When a route feels slow, there is often a smarter line that uses the new rule.

Who should play it

Suits OvO fans who want fresh ideas rather than more of the same. It also rewards puzzle-minded platformer players. Newcomers might prefer to learn the basics in the original first.
