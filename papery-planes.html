Papery Planes – Gentle hands keep the paper plane aloft

A folded paper plane and a course full of things to fly into. Papery Planes is a Unity WebGL reaction game about steering softly through obstacles. The motion is relaxed, yet hazards keep coming. Stay in the air as long as possible, and remember that small corrections do far more than big swerves.

The basics of Papery Planes

Guide the plane through a course that keeps throwing obstacles in its way. The plane glides forward, and you adjust its direction to slip through gaps. Touch an obstacle and the flight ends. The longer you fly, the better your result. The challenge is control, since overcorrecting is the usual reason for a crash.

Getting started

  • Make a short test flight to feel how quickly the plane turns.
  • Use small taps or gentle movements instead of sharp turns.
  • Look ahead to the next gap rather than the obstacle in front.
  • Restart quickly and try to beat your previous distance.

Tips from experience

Smooth is fast. Light inputs keep the plane steady and give you time to react. Pick a line through each gap early. Do not chase the center of every opening; aim for the safest path. Stay relaxed as the course gets busier.

Who should play it

Suits players who like calm reflex games and short attempts. It is easy to start. There is little variety beyond flying farther, so it works best in short sessions.
