Exo – Minimal puzzles that reward a calm plan

Clean shapes, empty space and nothing to distract you. Exo strips its puzzles down to the essentials so the only thing on screen is the problem. Each level is a small logical challenge. Look closely, form a plan and test it. When it fails, and it will, restart and try again with what you learned.

The basics of Exo

Every level presents a layout and a goal, and your moves change the state of the board. The rules are consistent, so once you understand how pieces behave, you can predict outcomes. Errors cost nothing but a reset, so experiment freely. Solving one level opens the next, and the difficulty rises gradually.

Getting started

  • Study the layout before making a single move.
  • Make a guess about the solution and test it.
  • Restart as soon as you see the plan cannot work.
  • Note any rule you discover; later puzzles build on it.

Tips from experience

Slow thinking pays here. Look at the goal state and work backward to find what must happen just before it. Count moves where possible; minimal puzzles often have tight solutions. Avoid trying random moves for long, as it hides the logic. If a level stumps you, take a break and come back with fresh eyes.

Who should play it

Suits players who enjoy quiet, abstract logic puzzles. The look is clean and calm. With no story or flair, it may feel dry to players who want more personality.
