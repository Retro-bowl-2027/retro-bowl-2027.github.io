Papa's Sushiria – Roll, slice, top and pour the bubble tea

Sticky rice, sheets of seaweed and a rolling mat sit at the heart of Papa's Sushiria, the series' sushi bar. Each ticket names a rice type, a wrapper, the fillings, the toppings and a bubble tea to go with it. Rolling tight and cutting even take a steady hand. Nail every detail and the tip jar fills.

The basics of Papa's Sushiria

After taking the order, move to the build station. Spread the requested rice over the wrapper, lay fillings in the listed order and roll it closed. Slice the roll into the number of pieces the ticket asks for, then add sauces and toppings. At the tea station, mix the drink and drop in the pearls. Serve both together for a tip based on accuracy.

Getting started

  • Confirm rice and wrapper choices before you begin rolling.
  • Lay fillings down in the sequence printed on the ticket.
  • Count the requested pieces before making the first cut.
  • Mix the bubble tea while another roll waits for its toppings.

Tips from experience

Cutting is the step that costs the most points. Space the cuts evenly and count as you go. Keep fillings in the middle so the roll seals properly. The tea is easy to forget, so start it right after rolling. Memorize what your regulars like to speed up.

Who should play it

Good for players who enjoy precise steps and a different cuisine. It has more parts than the early Papa's games. Players who dislike careful cutting may find the slicing frustrating.
