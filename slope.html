Slope – Steer the ball down a track that never ends

RobKaySF released Slope in September 2014, and the ball racing down a never-ending track has been a browser favorite ever since. The speed climbs constantly. Red blocks, sharp curves and open edges wait around every turn. Steer left or right, stay on the track and see how far you can roll.

The basics of Slope

The ball rolls downhill on its own and keeps getting faster. Steer to stay on the track and avoid red blocks, which end the run. Edges drop into empty space, and cliffs and curves keep changing the path. Survive as long as possible to push your distance score higher. Every run starts fresh from the top.

Controls

  • Left Arrow – go Left
  • Right Arrow – go right
  • Note – wASD keys also work

Getting started

  • Use small taps to keep the ball near the center.
  • Steer early into curves rather than at the last second.
  • Treat red blocks as walls and plan a path around them.
  • Restart right away and aim to beat your previous distance.

Tips from experience

Stay centered. The middle of the track gives you room to react in both directions. Anticipate turns by watching ahead. When the ball leaves the ground, steer gently in the air. At high speed, smaller inputs are safer. Calm hands go farther.

Who should play it

Ideal for players who love pure reflex challenges and score chasing. One key per direction makes it easy to learn. The endless format offers little beyond distance, so it suits short sessions.
