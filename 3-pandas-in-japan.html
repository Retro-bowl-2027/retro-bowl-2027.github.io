3 Pandas in Japan Online

3 Pandas in Japan – Three pandas, one puzzle per screen

A trio of pandas wanders through a hand-drawn version of Japan, and no obstacle on the way can be cleared by one of them alone. FlashTeam's 2018 puzzle hands you the mouse and asks you to make them work together. Each screen is a small riddle with a specific answer, and finding it means looking closely at everything drawn there.

The basics of 3 Pandas in Japan

You steer the group by clicking. Click the road to walk ahead, a click on empty air makes them hop, and a click on one panda piles it onto another. Clicks on parts of the scene can trigger other actions too. The goal of each level is to get all three pandas past the obstacle and onto the next screen. A wrong move usually just costs you a retry.

Controls

  • Clicking on Road – move Forward
  • Clicking on Air – jump
  • Clicking on Panda – stack
  • Note – clicking has other functionalities depending on where you click.

Getting started

  • Study the whole scene before you click anything.
  • Click scenery as well as the road; plenty of it responds.
  • Build a panda tower when a ledge or switch is too high.
  • Replay a failed level with the actions in a different order.

Tips from experience

Treat every level as a cause-and-effect puzzle. Something in the background usually matters, often an object you have not touched yet. Note what each click does and remember it, because similar tricks return later. Timing matters on some screens, so if the right action fails, try it a moment earlier or later. Avoid clicking frantically; it can send the pandas into trouble.

Who should play it

Good for younger players and anyone who likes gentle logic puzzles with no time pressure. Levels are short. The answers are fixed, so once a screen is solved there is little reason to replay it.
