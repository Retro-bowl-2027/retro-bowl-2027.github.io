circloO 2 – Reach each circle and watch the world grow

The sequel to circlO turns each level into an expanding map. You move a ball through a world of shapes, and every time it touches one of the target circles, the area opens up and new obstacles appear. Florian van Strien's 2018 follow-up keeps the minimalist look but adds more variety and a smoother sense of progress.

The basics of circloO 2

Arrow keys or WASD move the ball; Esc pauses. Your ball rolls across ramps, moving pieces and loose shapes. Touching a circle expands the map, revealing the next section and its challenges. Collect every circle in a level to complete it. Physics drives everything: weight, speed and the way shapes collide all decide your route.

Controls

  • Arrow / WASD Keys – move
  • Esc / Back – pause

Getting started

  • Explore each new section slowly after it opens up.
  • Use slopes to build speed for longer leaps.
  • Push loose shapes aside or use them as stepping stones.
  • Pause and study the layout when a section looks complicated.

Tips from experience

Each expansion changes the puzzle, so look at the whole new area before charging in. Momentum is your main tool; plan where you need speed and where you need control. Loose objects can block you or help you, depending on how you push them. If a circle seems out of reach, check whether an earlier part of the map offers a better approach.

Who should play it

A good pick for players who enjoyed the first game and for anyone who likes calm physics puzzles. It is more polished than the original. The minimalist style is the same, so it will not win over players who found circlO too plain.
