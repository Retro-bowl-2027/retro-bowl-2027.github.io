Mutazone Online

Mutazone – Scout the mutated zone, gear up, push deeper

Mutation has reshaped the world of Mutazone, and your character heads out to see what survived. This HTML5 RPG ties scouting, combat and loot into a steady cycle of getting tougher. Keyboard and drag input both work, so a touch screen is fine too. Preparation, more than luck, decides how far you get.

The basics of Mutazone

You roam zones crowded with hostile creatures and scattered loot. Battles and searching both turn up items that make your character stronger. Some zones are far more dangerous than others, and stumbling into one early can end badly. The aim is to scout each zone, beat what lives there and build a character ready for the next one. Read what each item does before relying on it.

Controls

  • Arrow Keys – move
  • Note – wASD keys and dragging also work

Getting started

  • Search your first zone from corner to corner before heading anywhere new.
  • Collect loot as you go and read its effect right away.
  • Pick easier opponents at first so your character gains strength safely.
  • Back off from any battle that is eating your health too fast.

Tips from experience

Get stronger before moving on. A zone that beats you now will feel easier after a few upgrades. Put new gear to use the moment you find it. Study each enemy's attack pattern and pick fights on your own terms. Remember which ground you have already covered, so you do not waste time crossing it twice.

Who should play it

Suits players who like steady RPG progress in short sessions. The pace is relaxed and the goals are clear. The story and systems are lighter than in large RPGs, so players wanting deep character builds may want more.
