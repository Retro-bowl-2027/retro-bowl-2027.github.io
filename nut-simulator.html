Nut Simulator – Click, upgrade, let the nuts pile up

Silly on purpose, Nut Simulator is an idle clicker that does not take itself seriously. The routine is classic: click to earn, spend earnings on upgrades, and watch the numbers climb. Some upgrades boost each click while others earn on their own. Your goal is to make the total grow faster and faster.

The basics of Nut Simulator

You start by clicking for small amounts. Spend what you earn on upgrades that either raise your per-click value or generate income automatically. Automatic income keeps building when you stop clicking, and the game keeps progressing while you are away. Upgrade costs tend to rise with each purchase, so choosing the right upgrade at the right time decides how fast you grow.

Getting started

  • Click actively at the start to afford the first few upgrades.
  • Buy an automatic earner early so income flows without clicks.
  • Compare each upgrade's cost with how much it adds before buying.
  • Check back after breaks and spend what built up while you were gone.

Tips from experience

Clicks matter early, automation matters later. Shift your spending toward automatic earners once they outpace your clicking. Avoid spreading money across every upgrade; focus on the best value. Spend idle earnings right away so they start working. Patience pays off more than fast clicking in the long run.

Who should play it

Suits players who like idle games and number growth in short check-ins. The humor is goofy and deliberately so. There is little strategy beyond choosing upgrades, so players wanting depth should look elsewhere.
