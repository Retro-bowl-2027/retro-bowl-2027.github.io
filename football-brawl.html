Football Brawl – Quick, rough football with goals over tactics

Football Brawl skips the tactics board and heads straight for the goal. InLogic Games built it as arcade soccer where action matters more than realism. Matches are short and busy. Chase the ball, battle your opponent for it and put it in the net more often than they do before the clock runs out.

The basics of Football Brawl

You control your player on a small pitch, moving to win the ball, shooting at goal and stopping the opponent from doing the same. Physical challenges are part of the game; contact is expected. The match runs on a timer, and the side with more goals at the end wins. Good positioning keeps you from conceding easy chances.

Getting started

  • Play a first match just to learn how fast your player moves.
  • Hold a line between the ball and your own net while defending.
  • Shoot early when the goal is open instead of dribbling closer.
  • Use challenges to win the ball, then move away quickly.

Tips from experience

Fast play beats fancy play. Win the ball, turn and shoot before your opponent recovers. Do not chase every loose ball if it leaves your goal empty. Angle your shots toward the corners. When you lead late, keep the ball near the opponent's end so they have to come through you to score.

Who should play it

Good for quick, chaotic soccer matches in a spare few minutes. The controls are easy. It offers little depth, so anyone after realistic football should look elsewhere.
