GunSpin – Fire the gun to send it flying farther

The gun is the hero, and recoil is its engine. Moosite SL's 2019 Unity game launches a firearm into the air and lets every shot kick it further along. Click at the right moment and the gun spins forward; click at the wrong one and it fires uselessly into the ground or sky. Distance is the goal.

The basics of GunSpin

Every click fires a bullet and pushes the gun in the opposite direction. Because the gun spins as it flies, the angle at the moment you shoot decides whether you gain height, distance or nothing at all. Ammo is limited, so each shot counts. When you run dry and the gun comes down, the run ends and your distance is recorded.

Controls

  • Click – shoot

Getting started

  • Watch the gun spin and learn when the barrel points backward.
  • Fire only when the barrel faces away from your direction of travel.
  • Save shots for when you are losing height.
  • Use any rewards between runs to improve your next attempt.

Tips from experience

Timing is everything. Shoot when the barrel points down and back to gain both height and distance. Firing while it faces forward pushes you backward and wastes ammo. Let the gun spin a little between shots rather than clicking rapidly. If upgrades are available, prioritize ammo and power for longer runs.

Who should play it

Good for quick, physics-based play with a funny concept. Runs are short. The idea is clever but thin, so it suits a few minutes rather than a long session.
