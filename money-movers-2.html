Money Movers 2 – The brothers return, and the guards are waiting

The brothers are back for another round of cooperative puzzles. Money Movers 2 keeps the two-player, one-keyboard setup and asks for tighter coordination than the first game. Levels mix switches and doors with guards you must get past. Timing your actions with your partner's becomes as important as finding the right route.

The basics of Money Movers 2

Both players must get their brother to the exit, collecting money along the way. Switches open doors, doors block paths, and guards stand between you and places you need to reach. A mistake sends you back to try the room again. Many rooms only work if both brothers act at the same moment or in a strict order. Planning before moving saves a lot of restarts.

Controls

  • Arrow Keys – move (Player 1)
  • WASD Keys – move (Player 2)

Getting started

  • Start with a quick talk about who controls which brother.
  • Study how each guard behaves before anyone steps into its area.
  • Agree on a count, such as three, two, one, for moves made together.
  • Split the level into steps and clear them one at a time.

Tips from experience

Guards make timing the real puzzle. Learn what each one does before you cross its ground. Count down out loud for actions that must happen together. When a room fails twice, stop and re-plan instead of rushing a third try. The player with less to do should watch the screen and call out danger.

Who should play it

Best for pairs who finished the first game and want harder rooms. The cooperative design rewards good communication. It is still a two-player game at heart, so solo players will find it awkward to control both brothers at once.
