Bob the Robber 2 Online

Bob the Robber 2 – Sneak past the guards and grab the loot

Bob is a thief with good manners and bad luck: every building he targets is crawling with guards, cameras and alarms. Meow Beast's 2012 stealth puzzle puts you in charge of getting him in and out unseen. Rushing gets him caught. Watching, waiting and moving at the right moment get him paid.

The basics of Bob the Robber 2

Each level is a building with a target to reach. Guards walk set routes, cameras sweep areas and alarms react to mistakes. You move Bob through rooms, doors and floors, and interact with objects that open or disable things along the way. Being spotted usually ends the attempt. Reach the goal undetected to finish the level.

Getting started

  • Watch each guard walk a full route before you move.
  • Time your crossing so a guard has just turned away.
  • Look for switches or devices that shut down cameras or alarms.
  • Use stairs and doors to put distance between Bob and patrols.

Tips from experience

Patterns are your map. Guards and cameras repeat their movements exactly, so a few seconds of observation tell you when each room is safe. Move during the gaps and stop in safe spots between them. Skip optional pickups that sit inside a guard's view. When caught, ask what you should have waited for, then wait for it.

Who should play it

Good for patient players who prefer puzzle stealth to action. Levels are short and logical. Players who want speed or combat will find the waiting slow.
