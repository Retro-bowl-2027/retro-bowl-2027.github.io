Copyright complaints (DMCA)

Game owners come first. If a page on Retro-Bowl-2027 uses work you own without your permission, tell us and we will act fast. In most cases we take the page down while we look into it.

Where to send it

Email contact@retro-bowl-2027.github.io and put "DMCA" in the subject line so it gets priority.

Checklist for a complete notice

  • Who you are: name, company, mailing address, phone and email.
  • What you own: identify the game or other work being copied.
  • Where it appears: the exact address of every Retro-Bowl-2027 page involved.
  • Your good-faith belief: a statement that the use is not authorized by you, your agent or the law.
  • Accuracy and authority: a statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the notice is accurate and that you own the rights or may act for the owner.
  • Your signature: typed or handwritten.

A notice missing one of these items can slow things down. We will reply and ask for what is missing.

If your page was removed

You can file a counter-notice at the same address. Give your contact details, say what was removed and where it was, and explain why you think the removal was a mistake. We pass valid counter-notices to the person who complained.

Want a small fix instead?

Many owners only want a credit corrected, a description tightened or a link to their own site added. No legal wording is needed for that. Send a plain email and we will make the change.
