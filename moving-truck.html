Moving Truck – Get the cargo there in one piece

A delivery only counts if the cargo arrives. Moving Truck gives you a loaded flatbed and a bumpy stretch of country between you and the drop-off. Hills, dips and potholes all try to bounce the boxes loose. Too much speed throws the load overboard. Too little and the truck stalls halfway up the next rise.

The basics of Moving Truck

Every stage is a single road over uneven ground with a finish point at the far end. Drive, reverse and tilt to keep the truck steady, and hold the nitro back for steep climbs. Getting there with the load still on board is what clears the stage. A jolt taken at a bad angle shifts the boxes or tips them off the back. One key press restarts the attempt.

Controls

  • Up Arrow – forward
  • Down Arrow – backward
  • Left / Right Arrow – tilt
  • Space – nitro
  • R – restart
  • Esc – pause
  • Note – wASD keys also work

Getting started

  • Crawl through the first stretch to see how the boxes slide around.
  • Use tilt to keep the bed close to flat over crests and hollows.
  • Hold nitro back for climbs your normal speed cannot handle.
  • Restart as soon as so much has fallen off that the stage is lost.

Tips from experience

Speed is the enemy of cargo. Approach bumps at a steady pace and ease off just before the top of a hill, so the load does not launch. Use tilt in the air to land on both axles. Nitro is strong but jerky; tap it instead of holding it when you carry a full load. Learn where each route's worst bump is.

Who should play it

Fits players who like physics driving with a careful, patient edge. Levels reward control over speed. Anyone who wants pure racing may find that protecting the cargo slows things down more than they would like.
