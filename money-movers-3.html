Money Movers 3 – Third outing, longer plans, closer teamwork

Chapter three raises the bar for teamwork again. The two brothers face fresh puzzles that only work when both players think a few steps ahead. Switches, doors and guards are all back, arranged in trickier ways. Money Movers 3 rewards teams who plan the full route out loud before anyone touches a key.

The basics of Money Movers 3

The goal stays the same: get both brothers to the exit and pick up money along the way. What changes is how much each step depends on the last. One brother may need to open a path the other uses moments later. Guards still punish careless moves. Each level is a compact puzzle, and the solution almost always has both players doing different jobs at once.

Controls

  • Arrow Keys – move (Player 1)
  • WASD Keys – move (Player 2)

Getting started

  • Name each obstacle in the level before deciding who handles it.
  • Let one player lead the plan while the other checks it for gaps.
  • Move in short stages and pause between them to confirm positions.
  • Go back for missed money once you know the safe route.

Tips from experience

Think in roles. One brother is often the opener and the other the runner, and swapping mid-level causes mistakes. Stop at each step of the plan and confirm both players are ready. Do not chase every coin on the first attempt. Finish the level, learn its logic, then go back for a cleaner result.

Who should play it

Aimed at teams with some experience of the series. New pairs can start here, but the earlier games teach the habits this one expects. As with the others, it depends on two players; solo play is possible but clumsy.
