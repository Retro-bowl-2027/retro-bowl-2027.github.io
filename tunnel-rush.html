Tunnel Rush Online

Tunnel Rush – Fly the neon tunnel and dodge everything

Deer Cat Games released Tunnel Rush in 2014, and its bright, spinning tunnel has hypnotized players ever since. You fly forward through a colorful tube while obstacles rush toward you. Steer left or right to slip through the gaps. The speed keeps climbing, and the vivid colors make it hard to stay focused.

The basics of Tunnel Rush

The tunnel stretches ahead, twisting and turning. Obstacles appear in your path, and you rotate around the tunnel to avoid them. Hit one and the run ends. Speed increases the longer you survive, and the colors shift to test your concentration. Survive as long as possible to beat your best score.

Getting started

  • Watch the first obstacles closely to learn their shapes.
  • Rotate early to line up with the gap ahead.
  • Keep your eyes on the far end of the tunnel.
  • Restart quickly after a crash to keep your rhythm going.

Tips from experience

Focus on the gap, not the obstacle. Small rotations work better than big swings, which tend to overshoot. When the colors shift, keep tracking the shapes rather than the light show. Take short breaks when your eyes start to tire. Rhythm comes with practice, and runs get longer fast.

Who should play it

Great for reflex fans who like fast, hypnotic games. Runs are short. The flashing colors may be uncomfortable for some players during long sessions, so pace yourself.
