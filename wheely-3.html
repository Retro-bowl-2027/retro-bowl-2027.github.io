Wheely 3 – More roadblocks for the little red car

Chapter three of the Wheely series lines up a new set of roadblocks for the little red car. Each scene is a tidy puzzle, and the answer comes from noticing how the pieces in it are linked. Nothing is timed and nothing is cruel. Look around, poke at things, and the road opens.

The basics of Wheely 3

Every scene shows the car on one side and its goal somewhere past a set of problems. Click parts of the scene to see what moves, opens or drops. Some items can be picked up and used elsewhere in the same picture. The order of your actions frequently matters, so a step done too soon can block the next. Get the car through safely to reach the next scene.

Getting started

  • Take in the whole picture before you click on anything.
  • Hunt for levers, buttons and anything that looks like it could move.
  • Decide the order of your actions before you send the car rolling.
  • Try a different sequence whenever a plan sends the car into trouble.

Tips from experience

Sequence is the hidden puzzle. Many scenes have the right pieces but need them used in a particular order. Watch for objects that change after you touch something else. There is no clock, so take your time with each picture. Small details, like a loose part or an odd shadow, often hold the answer.

Who should play it

Good for younger players and for anyone who likes friendly, low-pressure puzzles. Scenes are short and the art is cheerful. Seasoned puzzle fans may find the logic too gentle to slow them down.
