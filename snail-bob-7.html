Snail Bob 7 Online

Snail Bob 7 – Clear the path, because Bob will not wait

Hunter Hamster Studio released Snail Bob 7 in 2014, continuing the series about a snail who keeps moving forward on his own. You cannot steer Bob. Instead, you change the world around him: press buttons, pull levers and move objects to make a safe route. Get the timing wrong and Bob walks straight into danger.

The basics of Snail Bob 7

Each level is a small puzzle scene. Bob crawls from one side toward the exit. Click buttons, levers and objects to open paths, raise platforms or stop hazards. Bob does not pause for danger, so actions must happen at the right moment. Reach the exit to clear the level and move on.

Getting started

  • Look over the whole level before Bob gets far.
  • Click every button and lever once to see what it does.
  • Plan the order of actions so the route opens in time.
  • Restart quickly whenever Bob walks into trouble and try again.

Tips from experience

Timing is half the puzzle. Know which actions must happen before Bob arrives. Some objects can be used more than once, so try them again. Watch hazards move and pick your moment. Search each level carefully; small details often hide useful extras.

Who should play it

Ideal for puzzle fans who like gentle, clever levels. The cartoon style suits younger players. Levels are short, so experienced puzzle players may finish the whole game quickly.
