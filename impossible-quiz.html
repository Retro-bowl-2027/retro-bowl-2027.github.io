Impossible Quiz – Trick questions, absurd answers, three lives

Knowing facts will not help much. Slapp-Me-Do's 2007 Flash quiz built its reputation on questions designed to mislead, joke and confuse. Over a hundred of them stand between you and the end, and the answer that counts is often the silliest, or something not offered as an answer at all. You will fail a lot. You will also laugh a lot.

The basics of Impossible Quiz

Every question shows a few options; click the one you believe is right. A wrong pick takes a life, and running out of lives returns you to question one. Certain questions run on a timer, and others hide the solution somewhere other than the buttons, such as a detail in the picture. Skips picked up during play let you jump past a question that stumps you.

Getting started

  • Take each question exactly at its word; the phrasing hides the trick.
  • Scan the entire screen as well as the answer buttons.
  • Memorize answers after each failure, because the questions never change.
  • Hold your skips for the nastiest questions late in the run.

Tips from experience

Bend your thinking. Answers hinge on puns, double meanings and taking the question literally. When an option looks too easy, it may be bait, or it may be the joke. Memory carries you: each failure gives you an answer you will never miss again. On timed questions, stay calm and look for the hidden solution.

Who should play it

Great for anyone who loves absurd humor and lateral thinking. It is fun with friends shouting suggestions. Its trial-and-error design and restart on failure can frustrate players who prefer fair challenges.
