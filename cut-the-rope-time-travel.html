Cut the Rope Time Travel Online

Cut the Rope Time Travel – Two Om Noms, one rope puzzle, many eras

Om Nom climbs into a time machine and meets his ancestors. ZeptoLab's 2013 entry sends the candy-loving monster through historical eras, each with its own look and new gadgets to master. Many levels feature two Om Noms at once, present and past, and both want feeding. The rope-cutting basics remain, but the puzzles get busier.

The basics of Cut the Rope Time Travel

Drag to cut ropes and click to use special objects. The candy moves by physics, so cut order and timing shape its path. In some levels you must feed both Om Noms, which splits your attention and sometimes means handling two pieces of candy. New eras bring new mechanics that change how the candy travels. Collect stars as you go for a perfect score.

Controls

  • Drag – cut
  • click – action

Getting started

  • Identify every Om Nom in the level before your first cut.
  • Plan which candy goes to which monster before you cut.
  • Learn each era's new object in the first levels where it appears.
  • Collect stars only when it does not risk losing a candy.

Tips from experience

Two targets mean two plans. Solve the trickier delivery first, then deal with the simpler one while the other candy waits. Watch for interactions; one candy can knock the other off course. New objects in each era usually get a gentle introduction level, so experiment there. Most levels allow a moment to watch before cutting, so take it.

Who should play it

Ideal for fans of the series who want fresh mechanics and harder layouts. The time travel theme gives it variety. Newcomers may want to start with the original to learn the basics first.
