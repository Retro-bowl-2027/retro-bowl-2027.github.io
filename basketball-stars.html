Basketball Stars Online

Basketball Stars – Sixty-second duels decided by fakes and supers

A match lasts roughly a minute. Madpuffers built its 2019 hit around short, fast duels on a single basket. Beat your defender off the dribble, rip the ball away when they get careless and charge a super shot for the decisive moment. Solo tournament runs, head-to-head games on one keyboard and co-op games against bots are all on offer.

The basics of Basketball Stars

Arrows handle movement; tap a direction twice to sprint and press Up to leave the floor. Down doubles as a pump fake with the ball and a block without it. X is your shot or steal button, and Z fires a super once it has charged. Outscore your rival before the clock hits zero.

Controls

  • P – pause
  • Up Arrow – jump
  • Down Arrow – pump / Block
  • Left / Right Arrows – move
  • Double Left / Right Arrows – sprint
  • X – shoot / Steal
  • Z – super
  • W – jump (Player 1)
  • S – pump / Block (Player 1)
  • A / D – move (Player 1)
  • Double A / D – sprint (Player 1)
  • B – shoot / Steal (Player 1)
  • V – super (Player 1
  • Up Arrow – jump (Player 2)
  • Down Arrow – pump / Block (Player 2)
  • Left / Right Arrow Keys – move (Player 2)
  • Double Left / Right Arrow Keys – sprint (Player 2)
  • L – shoot / Steal (Player 2)
  • K – super (Player 2)

Getting started

  • Open with a solo match so the shot and steal buttons become second nature.
  • Shoot from spots that have worked for you and skip long, guarded attempts.
  • Fake with Down until your defender leaves the floor, then drive to the rim.
  • Hold the charged super back for a tight score or the last few seconds.

Tips from experience

Fakes win matches. Most defenders bite on a pump and float past you, leaving a clear path. When defending, keep your body on the line between ball and rim and swipe as the handler turns. Sprint in short bursts. Fire the super when it swings a close game rather than the moment it fills. Versus a friend, spot their favorite move and wait for it.

Who should play it

One of the best picks for two players sharing a keyboard, and solid alone too. Matches fit in a minute. The controls take a little practice, and a shared keyboard can feel cramped.
