Edge Let's Surf – The surfing game hidden inside a web browser

Microsoft hid this surfing game inside its Edge browser as an extra for anyone who went looking. Edge Let's Surf sends a surfer down a bright stretch of water dotted with rocks, buoys and other obstacles. It borrows heavily from the classic skiing game SkiFree, right down to a sea monster that gives chase.

The basics of Edge Let's Surf

Steer the surfer left and right to dodge hazards and pick up items such as boosts and extra lives. Speed builds the longer you ride. Run out of lives, or get caught by the creature in the endless mode, and the session ends. Other modes change the target, such as racing the clock or weaving through gates.

Getting started

  • Try the endless mode first to learn how the surfer turns.
  • Collect lives and boosts when they are close to your line.
  • Use a boost to outrun the creature when it gets close.
  • Switch to a timed mode once you can steer cleanly.

Tips from experience

Small steering inputs keep you fast. Wide turns cost speed and put you in the path of other obstacles. Look ahead for clusters and choose your line early. In endless mode, keep a boost saved for when the monster catches up. In timed modes, take the shortest line through gates even if it means passing close to hazards.

Who should play it

Ideal for a quick, cheerful break, especially for fans of retro arcade games. Each mode adds variety. It is lightweight, so players wanting depth will move on quickly.
