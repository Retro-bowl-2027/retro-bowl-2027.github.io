Santy is Home Online

Santy is Home – Holiday horror with an unwelcome festive visitor

A familiar festive figure has shown up somewhere he should not be. Santy is Home is a Unity WebGL horror game that twists the holiday season into something dark. You explore carefully, listen for sounds and avoid the threat. Atmosphere and tension do most of the work.

The basics of Santy is Home

Move through the setting while staying aware of the presence hunting you. Sounds give clues about where the threat is. Explore to find what you need while avoiding being caught. Moving slowly and planning ahead keep you safer than running. Survive and push forward through the experience. Rushing rarely ends well.

Getting started

  • Use headphones so you can hear where the threat is.
  • Move slowly through new areas and pause to listen.
  • Learn the layout so you know your escape routes.
  • Keep track of objectives so you do not wander aimlessly.

Tips from experience

Sound is your best tool. Stop and listen often. Avoid running unless you need to escape, since careless movement can lead you into danger. Plan routes before you move through dark areas. Stay calm; panic leads to mistakes. A slow, steady pace keeps you alive longer.

Who should play it

Suits horror fans who enjoy tension and atmosphere. The holiday twist gives it an unusual tone. It may be too frightening for younger players, and the WebGL build can be demanding on older computers.
