Ball Sort Halloween – Sort the spooky balls into matching tubes

A haunted coat of paint covers this version of the classic sorting puzzle. The logic is unchanged: every tube starts as a jumble, and you finish the level when each one holds a single kind of ball. The October theme changes the look and mood, while the rules stay exactly as you know them.

The basics of Ball Sort Halloween

Click a ball on top of a tube to lift it, then click a second tube to drop it there. A ball can only land on a matching type or in an empty tube, and each tube has limited room. Plan the order of your moves so you never block yourself in. The level is solved when every tube is full of one type or empty.

Controls

  • Click – sort

Getting started

  • Count how many of each ball type there are before moving.
  • Use empty tubes as temporary storage, not permanent homes.
  • Free the most buried type first if it blocks several tubes.
  • Start the level over when you are clearly stuck instead of forcing it.

Tips from experience

Think three moves ahead. Each move should complete a tube or open space, and never close off your options. Empty tubes are your most valuable resource, so fill one only with a clear plan for emptying it again. Look for tubes whose top balls already match the type you are building. Slow play solves levels faster than trial and error.

Who should play it

A gentle seasonal puzzle that suits all ages. It needs only clicks, so it works on any device. Players who already know ball sorting will find the theme is the only real difference.
