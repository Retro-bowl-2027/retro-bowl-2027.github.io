Blumgi Slime – Every leap counts when water waits below

One button and a blob of slime. Blumgi's platformer reduces the genre to a single action, jumping, and then makes that action surprisingly demanding. Each leap needs the right angle and the right amount of force to reach the next platform. Miss, splash into the water, and the run is over.

The basics of Blumgi Slime

Press Space or click to jump. The slime's direction and power depend on how and when you press, so landing on small ledges takes practice. Water below the platforms ends the attempt instantly. Reach the end of each level to unlock the next. A two-player mode puts both slimes on the course together, one jumping on A and the other on M.

Controls

  • Space Bar / Click – jump
  • A – jump (Player 1)
  • M – jump (Player 2)
  • R – restart

Getting started

  • Make a few practice jumps on solid ground to learn the arc.
  • Test small hops before attempting the long gaps.
  • Aim for the center of each platform, not its edge.
  • Press R to restart a level as soon as a jump goes wrong.

Tips from experience

Consistency is everything with one button. Find a rhythm that produces the same jump every time, then adjust in tiny steps. Land in the middle of platforms so a slight misjudgment does not tip you into the water. Pause before each jump; rushing causes most splashes. In two-player mode, ignore your rival and focus on your own timing.

Who should play it

Suits younger players and anyone who likes simple one-button challenges. It also works well for two people on one keyboard. Experienced players may find it short and the difficulty mild.
