Flappy Bird Online

Flappy Bird – Flap through the pipes and count every gap

Dong Nguyen's 2013 phone game became a global obsession because it was so unforgiving. 3kh0's browser version keeps the original idea intact. A small bird flies to the right, gravity drags it down, and every tap gives it a short lift. Squeeze through the gaps between green pipes. Touch anything and the run is over.

The basics of Flappy Bird

Each click or key press gives the bird one flap upward; stop and it drops. Pipes come in pairs from top and bottom, with the opening at a different height each time. Every pair you pass adds one point. There are no power-ups, no lives and no checkpoints. Your score is the number of gaps cleared before your first mistake.

Getting started

  • Tap in a steady rhythm to hold the bird at a level height.
  • Aim for the center of each gap, not its edges.
  • Let the bird sink slightly before tapping again for a lower gap.
  • Restart right away and keep sessions short to stay focused.

Tips from experience

Rhythm beats reaction. Find a tapping beat that keeps the bird flat, then adjust only as the next gap requires. Begin your climb or drop early; late corrections overshoot. Keep your eyes on the next pipe, not the bird. Expect to fail often. Scores above twenty come from calm, consistent taps, not frantic ones.

Who should play it

Fits anyone who loves a pure, brutal test of timing. One input makes it instantly playable. It is famously frustrating, so players who hate repeated failure should keep away.
