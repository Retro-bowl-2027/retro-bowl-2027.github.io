Stack Bump 3D Online

Stack Bump 3D – Scoop up the pile, keep it through the hazards

Your pile grows as you go, and the course seems designed to knock it over. Stack Bump 3D is a Unity WebGL timing game about scooping up pieces on the move while slipping past things that would scatter them. Each bump shaves some height away. The finish line rewards whatever you still carry when you arrive.

The basics of Stack Bump 3D

Travel down each course, gathering pieces simply by passing over them. Barriers and moving blockers sit along the route, and clipping one strips part of your pile. Good timing lets you grab more without losing it again. Cross the finish with a tall pile to score well and open the next course.

Getting started

  • Run the first course carefully to see how obstacles behave.
  • Grab pieces that sit on a safe line rather than risky ones.
  • Time your movement so obstacles pass before you cross their path.
  • Replay levels to find routes that collect more.

Tips from experience

A tall stack is only valuable if it survives. Skip a few pieces rather than risk a heavy loss. Learn the rhythm of moving obstacles and cross on the gap. Keep your eyes ahead to plan the next move. Consistency beats greed.

Who should play it

Suits players who like short, colorful timing games. Levels are brief and easy to replay. The core loop does not change much, which suits short sessions best.
