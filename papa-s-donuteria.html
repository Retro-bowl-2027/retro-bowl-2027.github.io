Papa's Donuteria – Cut, fry, fill and frost each donut

Dough goes in, donuts come out, and nothing gets served until the customer's design is matched. Papa's Donuteria sends you to a donut shop where each order specifies shape, filling and decoration. Cut the dough, fry it, fill it and frost it. Timing in the fryer matters as much as a steady hand with the icing.

The basics of Papa's Donuteria

Orders list the donut shapes, dough types, fillings and toppings. At the dough station, cut each shape from the right dough. In the fryer, cook both sides to golden without burning them. At the build station, add fillings, icing or drizzle and place toppings. Serve when everything matches. Tips grow with accuracy and pay for upgrades and ingredients.

Getting started

  • Match each donut shape on the ticket before cutting the dough.
  • Flip donuts in the fryer once the first side is golden.
  • Compare drizzle and topping placement with the ticket before serving.
  • Cut the next order's dough while the current batch fries.

Tips from experience

Fry in batches. Start several donuts at once so they finish together. Watch the color of each side separately. Icing coverage is scored, so cover each donut evenly. When orders queue up, finish one fully before starting the next to avoid mixing them up.

Who should play it

Suits players who enjoy precise decorating and timing. It has more hands-on detail than the early Papa's games. The fryer step can feel repetitive after many in-game days.
