About Retro-Bowl-2027

Here is the short version. Retro-Bowl-2027 lists 450 games that open in a browser tab, split across Action, Strategy, Casual, and Driving & sports. Press play and the game starts on the same page. No installer. No sign-up.

Why every game has a guide

A play button is not enough. Many browser games drop you in with no explanation, and the first few runs are spent guessing at the rules. We write a guide for each game so you can skip that part.

Each guide answers the same questions in the same order: what you do, how the controls work, what to do first, how to get better, and who the game suits. The tips are practical. They tell you what to press, what to watch and what to avoid.

How games are chosen

We add a game when it meets a few basic tests:

  • It loads reliably in current desktop browsers.
  • It is playable without payment or sign-up.
  • Its controls work as described and respond without lag.
  • It is suitable for a general audience.

How games are removed

We remove a game when it stops loading, breaks in common browsers, changes into something unsuitable, or when its owner asks us to take it down. If you find a broken game, tell us and include the page address.

How the site is funded

Retro-Bowl-2027 is paid for by advertising. Ads appear around the games and guides, never inside the guide text itself. Advertisers have no say in which games we list or what our guides say.

Who owns the games

The games are made and hosted by independent developers and studios. They own their games, names and artwork. Retro-Bowl-2027 owns the written guides and the way the library is organized. Game owners who want a change or a removal can read our DMCA page or write to us.

Contact

Questions, corrections and game suggestions go to contact@retro-bowl-2027.github.io. We read every message.
