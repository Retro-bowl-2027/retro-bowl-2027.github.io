Inifnite Soccer Online

Inifnite Soccer – Run the course, grab coins, finish with a penalty

AVIX Games blends a lane-runner with a spot kick. Your footballer sprints along a track strewn with barriers and coins, and the run ends at the penalty spot, where one good shot can multiply the whole haul. Infinite Soccer asks for quick feet first and a cool head last.

The basics of Inifnite Soccer

Up hops, Down drops into a slide, Left and Right switch lanes, and Space either boosts you or strikes the ball; WASD and drag input work too. Barriers sit at different heights, so each needs its own answer. Coins stack up along the way. When the run ends, a penalty against the keeper can raise your total, so an accurate finish pays.

Controls

  • Up Arrow – jump
  • Down Arrow – slide
  • Left / Right Arrow – dash Left / Right
  • Space – turbo / Shoot
  • Note – wASD keys and dragging also work

Getting started

  • Learn which barriers you hop and which you slide beneath.
  • Drift into coin-heavy lanes only when they are clear of barriers.
  • Fire the boost on open stretches to cover ground quickly.
  • Pick your spot carefully on the final kick, since it multiplies coins.

Tips from experience

Staying alive beats grabbing every coin. Never swerve into a barrier for a single pickup. Spot obstacles early and change lanes ahead of time. Keep some focus for the finish: a well-placed penalty can be worth more than everything before it. If coins unlock upgrades, choose the ones that lengthen your runs.

Who should play it

Fits casual players who like runners with a sports flavor. Runs are short and easy to repeat. Like most endless runners, it repeats itself after a while.
