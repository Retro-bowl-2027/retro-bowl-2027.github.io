Axis Football League Online

Axis Football League – Call the plays and run the team

Axis Games built this 2010 browser title around the thinking side of American football. You call plays, choose how to attack and decide how to defend. The game simplifies the sport enough to fit in a browser window, but every down still comes down to guessing what the other side expects and doing something else.

The basics of Axis Football League

Each possession is a series of downs. On offense, you pick a play, run or pass, and try to gain enough yards to keep the drive alive and reach the end zone. On defense, you choose how to line up and stop the other team. Managing your players is part of the job too. Win the game by scoring more points than your opponent.

Getting started

  • Learn the basic difference between run and pass plays before your first game.
  • Pick a few plays you understand and use them as your base.
  • Note which defensive calls stop the opponent and repeat them.
  • Check your team options before a match starts.

Tips from experience

Mix it up. A run-heavy offense gets read quickly, and so does one that passes on every down. Use short gains to set up longer attempts. On defense, track the plays your opponent likes and adjust after two or three snaps. Avoid risky passes when protecting a late lead. Field position matters more than one big play.

Who should play it

Suits football fans who care more about play calling than fast reflexes. It explains the sport's logic in a compact form. Graphics and depth show the game's age, and newcomers to the rules may need a primer first.
