Gun Mayhem – Knock rivals off the platforms with recoil and fire

Health bars do not matter in Kevin Gu's Flash shooter. Stick figures stand on a handful of floating platforms, and every bullet pushes its target backward. Shove an opponent off the edge and they lose a life. Gun Mayhem became a favorite for two-player battles because the idea is instantly clear and matches get chaotic fast.

The basics of Gun Mayhem

Move, jump and shoot to knock your opponent away; each weapon has its own fire rate and push. Fall off the stage and you lose a life, so staying on the platforms is as important as hitting your target. New weapons turn up during the match. The last player with lives remaining wins. Two people can battle locally, and computer opponents can fill out the arena.

Getting started

  • Learn how far a single shot pushes an opponent at close range.
  • Fight from the middle of a platform, never the edge.
  • Grab stronger weapons as soon as they appear.
  • Use your jump to recover when a hit knocks you into the air.

Tips from experience

Position before aggression. Shoot when your opponent is near an edge and you are safely inside. Recoil can work against you too, so be careful firing big weapons while standing at a ledge. Pressure opponents who just picked up a weapon before they can use it. Learn each stage's layout so you always know where safe ground is.

Who should play it

A classic for two players on one keyboard and for fans of chaotic arcade duels. Rounds are quick. Solo play against bots is fine but lacks the energy of a friend.
