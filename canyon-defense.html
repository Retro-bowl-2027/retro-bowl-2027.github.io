Canyon Defense Online

Canyon Defense – Hold the narrow passes with well-placed towers

Rock walls squeeze the enemy route into a handful of passages, and that shapes everything about Miniclip's 2008 tower defense. You cannot build anywhere you like. The canyon offers only a few good positions, and choosing them well matters more than buying the most expensive towers. Stop every wave before it breaks through.

The basics of Canyon Defense

Attackers file through the gorge wave after wave, and your towers wait on the rocks above to cut them down. Every kill pays, and the cash goes into new towers or improvements to the ones you have. Each tower type has its own strengths, so a balanced mix holds better than a row of identical guns. Leaks hurt, and later waves arrive tougher and faster.

Getting started

  • Before the first wave, find the ledges that overlook the longest stretch of route.
  • Put your opening towers where the path first twists.
  • Once the prime ledges are taken, pour money into upgrades.
  • Note which attackers survive longest and add a counter to them.

Tips from experience

One tower watching two passages beats two towers watching one. Narrow choke points squeeze attackers together, which is where splash damage shines. With so few build sites, upgrading becomes your main spending later on. Hold a small cash reserve so a surprise wave meets fresh firepower instead of an empty wallet.

Who should play it

Good for tower defense fans who enjoy placement puzzles and older Flash strategy games. The rules are familiar. It looks dated, and its pace may feel slow next to modern entries in the genre.
