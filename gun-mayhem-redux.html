Gun Mayhem Redux Online

Gun Mayhem Redux – The stickman shoving match, freshly polished

Redux is Kevin Gu's 2014 refresh of Gun Mayhem, and it keeps the formula that made the series popular for two-player battles: stick figures, floating platforms and guns that push harder than they hurt. The changes are about presentation and feel, giving the classic matches a cleaner look and smoother play.

The basics of Gun Mayhem Redux

Jump between platforms, scoop up weapons and fire to send your opponent flying. Every shot pushes the target, and every gun kicks back on you. Falling off the stage costs a life. The last fighter standing takes the round. The polish makes movement and shooting feel tighter, but the strategy stays the same: control the center and push others to the edge.

Getting started

  • Get a feel for movement on the first stage before picking fights.
  • Collect new weapons as soon as they appear on the stage.
  • Stay near the center of large platforms, away from the drop.
  • Push opponents toward the edges with steady, controlled fire.

Tips from experience

Map control decides most rounds. Hold the central platform and force opponents to come to you. Time shots for when your rival is mid-jump, since they cannot correct easily in the air. Do not chase across gaps unless your weapon has enough reach. Patience and position beat spraying bullets.

Who should play it

The best starting point in the series for newcomers who want the cleanest version. It suits local multiplayer best. Veterans of the original may find the changes modest.
