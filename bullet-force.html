Bullet Force Online

Bullet Force – A full multiplayer shooter in a browser tab

Console-style multiplayer, running in a browser. Blayze Games' 2016 shooter earned its reputation by offering what most web shooters skip: a long list of weapons, several maps and a choice of modes, all with real opponents. Join a match, pick a loadout and fight for kills and objectives in fast first-person battles.

The basics of Bullet Force

You play in first person against other people online. Move, aim with the mouse and fire, then reload and repeat. Matches follow familiar shooter modes, such as team battles and free-for-all fights. Earning kills and playing well unlocks more weapons and attachments for your loadouts. The team or player with the best result when the match ends wins.

Getting started

  • Join a team mode first so mistakes cost less than in free-for-all.
  • Pick a balanced rifle loadout until you know the maps.
  • Learn the main routes of one map before trying the others.
  • Check your mouse sensitivity in the settings before the first match.

Tips from experience

Map knowledge beats reflexes. Learn where players come from and hold positions that cover those lanes. Avoid open ground; move along walls and cover. Reload behind cover, never in the open. Keep your crosshair at head height as you walk so it is already close when an enemy appears. After each death, note where the shot came from and avoid that angle next time.

Who should play it

Ideal for players who want a competitive shooter without installing anything. The depth is closer to a console game than most browser titles. It needs a decent computer and connection, and new players face experienced opponents.
