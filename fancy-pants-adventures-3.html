Fancy Pants Adventures 3 Online

Fancy Pants Adventures 3 – The largest levels yet for the speedy stickman

By the third entry, Borne Games had a clear formula and much more space to play with. Fancy Pants Adventures 3, from 2012, delivers bigger levels and more secrets than the earlier games, with the same fluid, momentum-heavy movement at its heart. Exploring is as rewarding as finishing.

The basics of Fancy Pants Adventures 3

Run, jump and slide through sprawling levels, building speed on slopes to reach places you could never get to slowly. Enemies and obstacles test your control, while collectibles and hidden areas reward thorough players. Some routes only open with the right approach speed. Reach each level's end, or spend extra time uncovering everything it hides.

Getting started

  • Explore the first level thoroughly to learn how secrets are hidden.
  • Use slopes to build speed before tackling high or distant ledges.
  • Get past enemies without stopping whenever you can.
  • Return to finished levels later for anything you missed.

Tips from experience

Think in routes. Big levels have several paths, and the fastest often needs perfect momentum from a slope far behind. When you spot an unreachable area, look for a run-up that could carry you there. Keep moving through enemies by bouncing on them or slipping past. Explore high and low; secrets reward both.

Who should play it

Great for fans of the series and anyone who loves exploration-heavy platformers. It has the most content of the three. Players who prefer short, direct levels may find the size a bit much.
