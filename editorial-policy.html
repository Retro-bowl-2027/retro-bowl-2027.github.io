Editorial Policy

Every game on Retro-Bowl-2027 comes with a written guide. This page explains how those guides are built and the rules we follow.

The sections of a guide

All guides use the same structure, so you always know where to find what you need.

The basics

What you do in the game and what the goal is. One paragraph, no padding.

Controls

The keys, mouse actions or touch input the game uses.

Getting started

Three to five short steps for your first run, written as direct instructions.

Tips from experience

Practical advice for improving: what to prioritize, what to avoid and which habits pay off.

Who should play it

Who the game suits and why. If the game has a clear limitation, such as weak touch support or a slow start, we say so.

Game info

A short facts box with the developer, genre, release date and control type, where those are known.

Fact-checking

We base each guide on the game itself and on information from the developer. We check the controls, goals and core rules against the running game. We do not invent features, modes or numbers. When we are not sure about a detail, we leave it out rather than guess.

Corrections

Games get updated, and we make mistakes. If a guide is wrong or out of date, email contact@retro-bowl-2027.github.io with the page URL and the correct information. We check the claim and update the guide when it holds up.

Independence from advertisers

The site is funded by ads, but advertisers have no influence on our guides. They do not choose which games we list, how we describe them or what we say about their limits. Ads are kept apart from guide text, and no guide is paid for.
