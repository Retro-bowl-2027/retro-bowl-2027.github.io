Station 141 – Clear the corridors of a hostile space station

Tight corridors, sealed rooms and enemies waiting around every corner. Station 141 is a Unity WebGL shooter set aboard a space station, where you fight your way through deck after deck. There is little room to maneuver. Planning your route and keeping an eye on ammunition matter as much as quick aim.

The basics of Station 141

Move through the station's corridors and rooms, aiming and firing at the enemies you meet. Cover is limited, so use doorways and corners to break line of sight. Ammunition is not unlimited, so wasted shots hurt later. Clear each section to push deeper into the station. Some rooms hold more than one threat.

Getting started

  • Check every corner slowly before stepping into a new room.
  • Use doorways as cover when enemies open fire.
  • Count shots and reload before entering the next area.
  • Pick up any ammunition you find, even when you feel stocked.

Tips from experience

Corners are your friend. Peek, shoot and step back instead of charging. Fight one enemy at a time when you can. Keep your magazine topped up between fights. If a section goes badly, retry with a different order of rooms or a slower pace.

Who should play it

Good for players who like tense, close-quarters shooters. The station setting keeps things focused. The confined layout and Unity WebGL build may feel cramped or heavy on older computers.
