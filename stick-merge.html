Stick Merge – Combine fighters into a stronger army

Buy a fighter, buy another, merge them into something better. Stick Merge is a hugely popular merging game where your stick figure army grows stronger with every combination. Once the troops are ready, the battle mostly plays itself. The real skill is in building the right army before the fighting starts.

The basics of Stick Merge

Spend coins on new stick fighters and drag matching ones together to create a stronger version. Higher tiers have better weapons or stats. When your army is ready, start the battle and watch your fighters take on the enemy with little input from you. Win to earn more coins and keep upgrading.

Getting started

  • Buy the cheapest fighters early and merge them right away.
  • Merge pairs as soon as they match to free space.
  • Check your army's strength before starting a battle.
  • Spend winnings on new fighters to keep the merge chain going.

Tips from experience

Merge rather than hoard. Two weak fighters are worth less than one strong one. Fill the board efficiently so no slot sits empty. If a battle goes badly, build higher tiers before retrying. Focus on quality over quantity. Upgrading steadily beats rushing into fights.

Who should play it

Ideal for players who enjoy merge games and light strategy. Battles are quick. The fights need little input, so action fans may want more direct control.
