Riddle School Transfer 2 – The follow-up, with fresh jokes and puzzles

Five years after the first Transfer, Riddle School Transfer 2 picks the story back up. New puzzles, new jokes and new characters fill the follow-up. The rules of the series stay in place. Click around, collect what you find and solve problems the way a cartoon would. Creative thinking beats careful logic every time.

The basics of Riddle School Transfer 2

Explore each area by clicking on objects and characters. Gather items and use them to get past obstacles. Many puzzles take more than one step, so an item from one room may unlock progress in another. The humor holds the hints. Work through the chapter's challenges until you reach the end.

Getting started

  • Click on every object in a scene before moving to the next one.
  • Take note of characters who block your way and what they mention.
  • Carry items between rooms, since solutions often cross locations.
  • Retry failed ideas after picking up a new item.

Tips from experience

Treat jokes as clues. A pun or odd remark usually points to the next step. Track multi-step puzzles in order so you do not lose the thread. When everything seems tried, go back to the first area. Small details missed early often hold the answer.

Who should play it

Ideal for players who finished Transfer 1 and want the story to continue. It works best in one or two short sessions. As with the rest of the series, it leans on silly logic over challenge.
