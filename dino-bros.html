Dino Bros – Two dinosaurs, every coin, one exit

Two small dinosaurs, one level full of obstacles and a strict rule: no coin stays uncollected and nobody falls in. Martin Magni's puzzle platformer asks you to plan for both dinos at once. Gather every coin, bring the pair to the exit door, and keep them well away from the water.

The basics of Dino Bros

Move with the arrow keys or WASD. Coins are scattered across the level, and you cannot advance until all of them are collected. Water is deadly; if either dinosaur falls in, the attempt is over. Levels mix platforms, gaps and obstacles that need careful sequencing, so think about where each dinosaur needs to be before you move.

Controls

  • Arrow / WASD Keys – move

Getting started

  • Look over the whole level and count the coins first.
  • Find the water hazards and plan routes around them.
  • Move one careful step at a time near edges.
  • Plan the order of coin pickups before heading for the door.

Tips from experience

Two characters double the room for error. Before each move, check where both dinosaurs will end up, especially near water. Collect the hardest coins first while you still have the most options. If a level feels impossible, try a different coin order. Slow, deliberate movement beats speed every time here.

Who should play it

Good for players who like co-op style puzzles and planning. Levels are compact. The difficulty is modest, so seasoned puzzle fans may finish quickly.
