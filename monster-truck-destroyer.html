Monster Truck Destroyer Online

Monster Truck Destroyer – Giant wheels, heavy frame, flatten the course

Gametornado put you behind the wheel of an oversized monster truck in this 2013 driving game. Parked cars and junk that would stall a family sedan are speed bumps here. Each stage is a route to the finish. Raw power is on your side. Keeping a tall, top-heavy machine upright is the part that takes skill.

The basics of Monster Truck Destroyer

You pilot a heavy truck across stages littered with rough ground, abandoned cars and other junk. Those enormous tires let the truck climb straight over most of it, yet the weight up top means steep slopes and sloppy landings can roll it over. Get to the finish without wrecking to unlock the next stage. Flattening everything along the way is half the appeal.

Getting started

  • Feather the gas for the first few seconds to sense how much the truck weighs.
  • Crawl over small piles so the frame stays even.
  • Brake ahead of big drops so the truck does not land upside down.
  • Reset the stage as soon as the truck wedges itself at a bad angle.

Tips from experience

Flooring it rarely works. Meet obstacles at a constant pace so the tires bite and carry the frame over. On climbs, keep an eye on the nose; if it rises too steeply, lift off the gas. Square up before ramps so all the tires hit the ground together. Figure out where each stage's nasty spots are and set your speed for them in advance.

Who should play it

Suits players who like heavy vehicles and simple, destructive driving. Stages are short. Anyone after realistic racing or detailed vehicle setup will find it thin.
