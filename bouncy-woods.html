Bouncy Woods – Bank your shot off the trees and hit the mark

A friendly forest, a bouncing ball and targets placed where a straight shot will never reach. Robowhale's 2020 physics game turns the woods into a giant pinball table. Trees, walls and other surfaces all send the ball in new directions, and the trick is to plan those rebounds before you let go.

The basics of Bouncy Woods

Drag to set direction and power, then release to fire. The ball ricochets off obstacles and keeps moving until it comes to rest or leaves play. Hit the targets in each level to clear it. Many targets hide behind cover, so the best shots use one or two bounces on the way. Clean, efficient shots are the goal.

Controls

  • Drag – aim
  • Release – shoot

Getting started

  • Fire one soft test shot to see how much the ball bounces.
  • Look for a surface that angles toward the target.
  • Change only the angle or only the power between shots.
  • Hit exposed targets first, then work on the hidden ones.

Tips from experience

Think like a pool player. A ball leaves a surface at roughly the angle it arrived, so you can trace a bank shot with your eyes before firing. Softer shots are easier to predict, because hard ones bounce many times and drift. Use corners for steep redirects. When a level feels impossible, look for the surface you have not tried yet.

Who should play it

Good for players who enjoy angle puzzles and calm, cute visuals. Levels are short. The difficulty stays gentle, so puzzle veterans may finish it quickly.
