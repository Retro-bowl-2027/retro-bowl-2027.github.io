Bloons TD 4 Online

Bloons TD 4 – Upgrade paths and blimps raise the stakes

Bloons TD 4 is where the series found its depth. Ninja Kiwi's 2009 entry offers a wide roster of monkey towers, each with upgrades that change how it fights, and a large cast of balloon types designed to beat a lazy defense. Camouflaged balloons, armored ones and giant blimps test every build you try.

The basics of Bloons TD 4

Set towers along the track, start each wave and earn money from every pop. Spend it on new monkeys or on upgrades that add range, speed or special abilities. Different balloons need different answers: some are invisible to most towers, others shrug off sharp attacks. Leaks cost lives. Clear every round on a track to win, then try a harder difficulty.

Getting started

  • Start on an easy track and difficulty to learn the tower roster.
  • Place dart monkeys early for cheap, reliable popping.
  • Add a tower that can detect camouflaged balloons before they appear.
  • Upgrade a few strong towers instead of buying many weak ones.

Tips from experience

Plan for the special balloons before they show up. Have something that pops lead and something that sees camo in place a few rounds early. Upgrades often beat new purchases, because a fully improved tower outperforms several basic ones. Use bends and loops for maximum coverage. Before big waves, check where leaks happened last time and reinforce that spot.

Who should play it

A great entry point for tower defense fans and a highlight of the series. The depth rewards replays across tracks and difficulties. Its interface feels dated next to newer entries, though the strategy holds up well.
