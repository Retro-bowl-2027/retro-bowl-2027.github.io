GOODNIGHT – A sleepy platformer that rewards a gentle pace

Most platformers shout. GOODNIGHT whispers. deepsleep's 2018 HTML5 game wraps its jumps and hazards in a soft, drowsy mood, the sort of game that suits a dim room at the end of the day. The challenge is genuine, though, and it pays off careful eyes and unhurried timing far more than haste.

The basics of GOODNIGHT

Lead your character through each stage, hopping past hazards and working out a way onward. Stages are designed to be studied first and played second; hazards keep to patterns you can memorize. A slip sends you back, so a patient approach saves time overall. Each completed stage takes you further into the game's hushed, dreamy world.

Getting started

  • Pause at the start of each stage and take in the whole screen.
  • Let every hazard complete one full cycle before you leap.
  • Edge forward near drops rather than sprinting at them.
  • Play somewhere quiet so the mood can do its work.

Tips from experience

Slow is smooth. Panicked leaps get punished far harder than measured ones. Study a section, choose a landing spot and commit to it. When one hazard keeps catching you, stop and observe it again before the next attempt. Approach the game as a way to wind down rather than a race, and it gives back more.

Who should play it

Suited to players who want a relaxed but still engaging platformer. It has a distinctive calm mood. Players looking for speed or intense action may find it too quiet.
