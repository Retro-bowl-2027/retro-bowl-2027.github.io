Bloons TD – Where the monkeys-versus-balloons series began

Every Bloons Tower Defense game traces back here. Ninja Kiwi's 2007 original set up the premise a whole series would build on: a line of colorful balloons floats down a winding track, and dart-throwing monkeys stand guard beside it. The tower selection is small and the art is basic, yet the central puzzle is already sharp.

The basics of Bloons TD

Buy a monkey, drop it next to the track, and send the wave on its way. Every balloon popped adds to your funds for more defenses. Later waves bring tougher balloons, and popping one often reveals a weaker one inside. Anything that slips past the last tower takes away lives. Lose them all and the run is over; clear every round to beat the track.

Getting started

  • Place your first monkeys where the track loops near itself.
  • Start the first round early, since early waves are weak.
  • Spend each round's earnings right away on new defenses.
  • Watch which balloons slip through and cover that stretch.

Tips from experience

Coverage at bends is your foundation. A monkey placed inside a curve hits balloons on two or three stretches of track. Layer different defenses so balloons pass through several zones in a row. Do not rely on a single tower type; later waves punish a one-note plan. Keep an eye on lives and fix leaks the moment they appear.

Who should play it

A neat history lesson for Bloons fans and a simple tower defense for newcomers. It runs on almost anything. Players used to later entries will miss the deep upgrade trees and wide tower choice.
