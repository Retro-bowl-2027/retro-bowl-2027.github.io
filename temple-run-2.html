Temple Run 2 Online

Temple Run 2 – Turn, jump, slide and outrun the beast

Imangi Studios shipped Temple Run 2 in January 2013, and the sequel became a fixture of the endless runner genre. This browser take keeps the premise. An explorer sprints along crumbling cliff paths and jungle ruins with a huge beast thundering behind. Bends arrive without warning, and so do gaps, low branches and broken walkways. The run lasts as long as your reflexes do.

The basics of Temple Run 2

Your explorer keeps sprinting forward on their own. Swing left or right where the path bends, leap over breaks in the walkway and duck under anything hanging low. Gold coins sit along the route, and pickups can protect you or multiply your haul. Miss a bend or clip a barrier and the beast catches up. Every attempt begins again from the start.

Getting started

  • Get the feel of cornering before you start chasing coins.
  • Leap breaks in the path and duck under branches and low beams.
  • Grab pickups when they sit on the line you were taking anyway.
  • Keep your gaze down the path, because bends come up fast.

Tips from experience

Read the path ahead instead of watching the runner. At top speed you get a fraction of a second per obstacle, so commit to each move cleanly. Coins matter less than survival; skip a row if it means a risky lane. Keep your hands loose as the pace climbs, since stiff, late inputs cause most missed corners.

Who should play it

Ideal for anyone who enjoys endless runners and remembers the mobile hit. Starting takes seconds. Imangi owns the brand, and this browser version is based on the idea, so details may differ from the official app.
