Super Liquid Soccer – Jelly bodies, a squishy ball and wobbly goals

Everything wobbles in Super Liquid Soccer. Bodies squash on contact, the ball deforms when struck, and shots curl in ways no real pitch would allow. Punyrobot leaned into that squishiness on purpose, and it keeps matches light and silly. Getting a feel for how the gooey physics react is what separates lucky goals from earned ones.

The basics of Super Liquid Soccer

Run your player around the pitch, strike the ball and try to put it past the other side. Because everything is soft, contact rarely goes exactly to plan: the ball can squish off a boot or rebound oddly off a body. Be ahead on goals when time expires. Every match teaches you a bit more about how the goo behaves.

Getting started

  • Play a few touches to see how the ball reacts to your kicks.
  • Approach the ball from behind to aim shots more reliably.
  • Stay near your goal when the opponent has the ball.
  • Shoot when you are close, since long shots wobble off target.

Tips from experience

Keep it simple. Short, controlled kicks land better than big swings. Expect odd bounces and position yourself to react to them. Use your body to block shots on your goal. Learn the physics in early matches, and the chaos becomes far easier to manage.

Who should play it

Suits players who like silly, physics-driven sports games. Matches are quick and goofy. Players wanting precise soccer controls may find the jelly feel frustrating.
