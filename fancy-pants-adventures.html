Fancy Pants Adventures Online

Fancy Pants Adventures – Ride the slopes in the orange pants

A stick figure in bright orange trousers races across hand-drawn hills, and the way he moves is the reason people still remember this game. Borne Games' 2006 Flash platformer is built on momentum. Run down a slope and you pick up real speed; carry it into a jump and you fly. Levels hide secrets for players who explore.

The basics of Fancy Pants Adventures

The basic verbs are run, jump, slide and kick off walls. Terrain shape is the real control: rolling down an incline piles on speed that carries you through the next jump, and climbing bleeds it away. Squiggles dotted around the world are the collectibles, and tucked-away rooms reward anyone who wanders. Enemies can be stomped or sidestepped. Finish a level quickly or comb it for everything it hides.

Getting started

  • Let the first downhill show you how much speed a slope gives.
  • Take jumps at full pace rather than from a standing start.
  • Learn the wall jump in a narrow shaft where two walls face each other.
  • Leave the obvious route now and then to hunt for hidden rooms.

Tips from experience

Speed is how this game talks. Line up each big jump with a descent before it. Slide under low ceilings to keep your pace. When no slope helps, a wall jump gets you height. Replay levels you know; some secret areas only open when you arrive fast and from the right side.

Who should play it

A classic for platformer fans and anyone curious about Flash-era highlights. The movement still feels great. It is short, and the first game in the series is lighter on content than the sequels.
