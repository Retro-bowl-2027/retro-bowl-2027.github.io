G-Switch 2 – Gravity flipping returns with tougher courses

Five years after the first game, Serius Games brought its gravity-flipping robot back with new courses and a steeper challenge. G-Switch 2 keeps the one-key control that made the original so easy to start. The difference is in the level design, which now places obstacles on floor and ceiling alike and leaves much less room for error.

The basics of G-Switch 2

Any key flips gravity, and Esc pauses. The robot runs on automatically, and the course throws gaps, blocks and hazards at both surfaces, so neither the floor nor the ceiling is safe for long. Timing each flip to slip between obstacles is the whole game. Keep running as far as you can before the robot falls or gets left behind.

Controls

  • Any Key – flip Gravity
  • Esc – pause

Getting started

  • Warm up with a few easy flips before chasing distance.
  • Scan both surfaces, since hazards now appear on each.
  • Flip in the middle of a safe stretch to prepare for the next threat.
  • Stay calm when obstacles alternate quickly between floor and ceiling.

Tips from experience

Think in sequences. Sections often alternate hazards between surfaces, so plan two or three flips at once. Avoid unnecessary flips; every switch exposes you to new risks. Watch the spot where you will land, not where you are now. If a section keeps beating you, focus on its first move, since the rest usually follows.

Who should play it

Good for players who enjoyed the original and want a harder version. It is still one key and instant to learn. The difficulty jump can frustrate newcomers, who may prefer to start with the first game.
