Pacman Online

Pacman – Clear the dots, turn the tables on the ghosts

Dots, ghosts and a maze with no room for hesitation. This unofficial browser Pacman follows the classic setup: steer the yellow hero around the maze, eat every dot and stay away from the ghosts. Power pellets flip the chase for a few seconds. Clear the board to move on.

The basics of Pacman

You move through a maze lined with dots. Eat them all to clear the level. Ghosts roam the corridors, and touching one costs a life. A power pellet makes the ghosts vulnerable for a short time, and eating them then earns big points. Lose all your lives and the game ends.

Controls

  • Arrow Keys – move
  • P – pause

Getting started

  • Clear the dots far from the ghosts first while the maze is calm.
  • Save power pellets until ghosts are close enough to catch.
  • Press P to pause when you need a moment to plan.
  • Learn the corridors so you always know your next escape route.

Tips from experience

Keep moving; stopping invites a trap. Lure ghosts near a power pellet, then eat it and turn on them. Corners slow you down, so plan routes with fewer turns when chased. Leave a few dots near a pellet for last; they are easy to grab while the ghosts are vulnerable.

Who should play it

Suits anyone who enjoys classic arcade games and quick sessions. The rules are known worldwide. As an unofficial version, details may differ slightly from the original arcade machine.
