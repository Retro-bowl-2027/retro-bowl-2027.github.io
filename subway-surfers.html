Subway Surfers Online

Subway Surfers – Dash the tracks and dodge the trains

SYBO's Subway Surfers, first released in May 2012, became one of the most famous mobile games ever. This browser version captures the idea: a young runner escapes a guard and his dog by sprinting along busy railway tracks. Jump, roll and change lanes to avoid trains and barriers. Collect coins and power-ups as you go.

The basics of Subway Surfers

The runner moves forward automatically along three lanes of track. Switch lanes to avoid oncoming trains and obstacles, jump over barriers and roll under others. Coins line the tracks. Power-ups and a hoverboard can help you survive a crash. Hit an obstacle without protection and the guard catches you. Run as far as possible.

Controls

  • Up Arrow – jump
  • Down Arrow – roll / Smash
  • Left / Right Arrows – dash Left / Right
  • Space – activate Hoverboard
  • Esc – pause

Getting started

  • Practice switching lanes cleanly before attempting tricky jumps or rolls.
  • Roll under high barriers and jump over low ones.
  • Use the hoverboard when the tracks get crowded.
  • Collect coins only when they sit on a safe path.

Tips from experience

Look ahead, not at the runner. Trains and barriers come fast, so plan lane changes early. Stay on top of trains when possible; the view is better up there. Save the hoverboard for dangerous moments. Calm, steady input keeps runs going longer.

Who should play it

Ideal for fans of endless runners and the mobile classic. It is easy to start. As a browser version of the idea, it may lack some features of the official mobile app.
