Adrenaline Challenge Online

Adrenaline Challenge – Grab every green dot, then ride out

Each course hides a set of green dots, and the exit stays shut until you have every one. Blackmaze's 2009 motorcycle trials game builds its levels from hills, drops and ramps that try to flip you at every turn. Speed helps only until you land badly. Balance is the real skill here.

The basics of Adrenaline Challenge

You ride a dirt bike across side-view terrain, using the keyboard for throttle, brake and the lean of the rider. Leaning forward or back changes how the bike climbs and lands. The task in every level is the same: collect all green dots scattered around the course, then return to the exit. Crashing ends the attempt, so a smooth route counts for more than a fast one.

Getting started

  • Ride the level slowly once to find where every green dot sits.
  • Plan a route that collects the dots in one loop toward the exit.
  • Lean back on climbs and forward on descents to stay upright.
  • Land with both wheels level whenever you can.

Tips from experience

Most crashes come from too much throttle on a ramp. Ease off before the lip and let the bike's momentum carry you. Adjust the lean in the air with short taps instead of holding the key down. If a dot sits on a steep slope, approach it slowly from below. On unfamiliar ground, choose safety over speed every time.

Who should play it

Fits fans of physics-based bike games who like learning one level until it clicks. Each course is short. The trial-and-error loop can frustrate players who prefer steady progress.
