Blumgi Rocket – Charge the rocket, aim the car, fly to the finish

A small car, a big rocket strapped to its back and a finish that is never simply straight ahead. Blumgi's cheerful physics game builds each level as a short course of ramps, gaps and odd terrain. Driving gets you into position. The rocket, charged and fired at the right angle, does the rest.

The basics of Blumgi Rocket

Arrow keys or WASD drive the car and tilt it to set your aim. Holding Space charges the rocket; letting go fires it and sends the car flying in the direction it faces. A stronger charge means a bigger launch. Each level is won by reaching the finish. Press R to restart instantly when a launch goes wrong, which it often will at first.

Controls

  • Arrow / WASD Keys – move
  • Space – charge Rocket
  • R – restart

Getting started

  • Drive to the edge of each ramp before you start charging.
  • Tilt the car so its nose points where you want to fly.
  • Charge partway on your first try, then adjust the strength.
  • Hit R the moment a launch misses rather than waiting it out.

Tips from experience

Angle matters more than power. A full charge pointed slightly wrong overshoots everything, while a modest charge at the right angle lands softly. Watch how the car rotates in the air and correct with small taps. Use the terrain, since a ramp adds lift that a launch from flat ground cannot. Restarting is free, so treat early attempts as measurements.

Who should play it

Great for kids and casual players who enjoy quick physics puzzles. Levels are short. Experienced players will clear it quickly, as the difficulty stays gentle.
