Ball Sort Soccer – A sorting puzzle in a soccer kit

Footballs replace the usual colored marbles in this sports-themed sorter. Every tube begins with a scramble of different ball designs, and you shuffle them until each tube carries just one design. Nothing about the logic changes with the theme, so anyone who has sorted balls before will feel at home within seconds.

The basics of Ball Sort Soccer

A first click lifts the uppermost ball from a tube; a second click places it on another. The ball is accepted only by a tube whose top ball has the same design, or by one that is empty. Space inside each tube is tight. Every stage tests how well you order your moves, and it ends when each design has a tube to itself.

Controls

  • Click – sort

Getting started

  • Learn to tell the ball designs apart; some look alike at a glance.
  • Keep a single tube free to use as a parking spot during tricky shuffles.
  • Complete one design fully before you start on another.
  • Count the open slots in a tube before you stack onto it.

Tips from experience

Similar-looking designs cause most errors, so slow down until you can identify each one instantly. Scan the whole board and choose the move that frees the most stacks. Never fill your last empty tube with a mix. If a stage stalls, start it again and open with a different tube this time.

Who should play it

Fits soccer fans and casual puzzlers looking for a calm few minutes. Everything is done with clicks. Apart from the football designs, it plays like every other ball sort game.
