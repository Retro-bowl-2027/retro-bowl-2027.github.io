Doge Miner Online

Doge Miner – Dig for virtual coins and aim for the moon

A Shiba Inu with a pickaxe and a dream. rkn's 2014 clicker turns the Dogecoin meme into an idle loop: click to dig, collect virtual coins, buy helpers that mine for you. The goal follows the old crypto joke about going to the moon. No real currency is involved; every coin exists only in the game.

The basics of Doge Miner

Each click digs and earns coins. Spend them on upgrades that raise your digging power or add miners that work automatically. Better helpers cost more but produce much more, and the shop grows as your earnings rise. Saving up for the bigger purchases eventually takes the operation further afield. Progress is a steady climb from single clicks to a busy mining business.

Getting started

  • Click steadily at the start to buy your first automatic miner.
  • Favor upgrades that raise automatic income over click upgrades.
  • Save for the next big helper when its payback is worth the wait.
  • Check back regularly to spend coins as they pile up.

Tips from experience

Automatic income compounds, so it deserves most of your money. Work out roughly how long each purchase takes to pay for itself and buy the fastest one. Clicking matters early but fades later. Avoid sitting on coins without a plan. When a new tier appears, compare it with stacking more of the current one before choosing.

Who should play it

Fits fans of idle games and internet humor. It is light and easy to dip in and out of. Like most clickers, it becomes slow and repetitive once the main upgrades are bought.
