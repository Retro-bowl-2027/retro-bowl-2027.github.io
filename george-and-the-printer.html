George and the Printer – Outsmart one very stubborn office printer

Every office has a printer that refuses to cooperate. Robin Six built a whole escape-style puzzle around one. George and the Printer takes a painfully ordinary setting and fills it with odd situations, and the contrast is where the humor comes from. Look around, collect useful objects and figure out how to get the machine to do its job.

The basics of George and the Printer

Explore the scene by clicking on things. Some objects can be picked up, others react when you use an item on them. The puzzles are about combining what you find in the right order and the right place. Each solved step moves the story forward. The tone is light, and many solutions are as silly as the problem.

Getting started

  • Click on everything in the scene to see what can be picked up.
  • Open your inventory after each discovery to see what you hold.
  • Try using items on the printer and on other objects nearby.
  • Think about what a frustrated office worker would actually try.

Tips from experience

Escape puzzles reward thoroughness. Search every corner of the scene before assuming you are stuck. When an item seems useless, try it on every object, including the printer itself. Look for hints in background details, such as text in the scene. If all else fails, think of the silliest possible solution; this game often uses it.

Who should play it

Good for players who enjoy short, funny point-and-click puzzles. It is light and approachable. Expect a compact puzzle rather than a long adventure.
