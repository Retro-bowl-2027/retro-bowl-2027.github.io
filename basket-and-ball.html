Basket and Ball Online

Basket and Ball – Roll the ball through the course and sink it

Here the ball is the hero. Sun Temple's 2015 game blends platforming, physics puzzles and a little shooting, and puts a basketball in charge of its own fate. Each level is an obstacle course that ends at a hoop. Get the ball there, fall through the net, and grab the stars scattered along the way if you can.

The basics of Basket and Ball

The arrow keys roll the ball sideways, and Space gives it an extra hop. Courses throw gaps, slopes, moving pieces and hazards at you. Dropping through the net completes the level. Stars are optional extras for players willing to take risks. A few stages switch to shootouts: there you pull back with the mouse to line up a throw and let go to fire at the basket.

Controls

  • Left / Right Arrow Keys – move
  • Space – bounce
  • Drag – aim
  • Release – shoot
  • Note – only drag and shoot when at Shootout Challenges

Getting started

  • Get a feel for how high Space makes you bounce on flat ground.
  • Collect stars only when the route to them is safe.
  • Slow down before ramps and gaps to control your landing.
  • In shootout stages, drag gently and adjust after each attempt.

Tips from experience

Momentum is the tricky part. The ball keeps rolling after you let go, so tap the arrow keys rather than holding them near edges. Time bounces at the top of a slope for extra height. Go for the hoop first on a new level, then replay for stars once you know the layout. In shootouts, keep the same drag length and change only the angle.

Who should play it

Good for kids and casual players who like light platforming with a sports twist. Levels are bite-sized. Experienced platformer fans may find it easy and a little slow.
