Champion Island Online

Champion Island – Explore the island and beat the seven champions

Google's 2021 Doodle for the Tokyo Olympics grew into a full retro adventure. You play Lucky, a cat athlete who arrives on an island ruled by legendary champions. Explore, talk to the locals, take on side quests and challenge each champion in their sport. The art borrows from classic Japanese games and folklore.

The basics of Champion Island

Walk around the island with the arrow keys and use Space to interact or act in events. Each area hosts a sport, including table tennis, skateboarding and archery, with more to discover. Beating a champion earns their sacred scroll. Characters around the island offer side quests. Collect every scroll to finish the main story while your chosen team climbs the leaderboard.

Getting started

  • Pick a team at the start; your results feed its global score.
  • Explore the starting area and talk to characters before your first sport.
  • Read each sport's instructions before the event begins.
  • Return to sports later to improve your times or scores.

Tips from experience

Each sport is a small game with its own timing, so treat early attempts as practice. Watch the champion's pattern and react rather than mashing Space. Side quests reward exploration and add story, so do not rush straight to the champions. Progress may reset if you clear your browser data, so finish what you can in one sitting.

Who should play it

Great for families, kids and fans of retro RPGs. Each event is quick and the controls are just arrows and Space. The sports are simple, so players seeking a deep challenge will finish quickly.
