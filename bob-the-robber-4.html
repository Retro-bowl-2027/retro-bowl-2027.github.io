Bob the Robber 4 Online

Bob the Robber 4 – New buildings, new security, the same careful thief

The fourth chapter brings Bob back with a fresh list of targets. Meow Beast sends him through new locations protected by smarter security, and each one adds devices that break the habits you learned earlier. The rule is unchanged: observe first, move second, and never get seen.

The basics of Bob the Robber 4

Every level presents a building and a goal inside it. Guards patrol, security devices watch or block passages, and Bob must slip through without triggering anything. You can hide when danger approaches and wait for patrols to pass. Some doors and devices must be dealt with before the path opens. Get caught and the level starts over.

Getting started

  • Stand still and study the room before moving Bob.
  • Find hiding spots along your route before you need them.
  • Learn what each new security device does the first time you meet it.
  • Cross open areas only in the gaps between guard patrols.

Tips from experience

Treat each room as a timing puzzle. Note how long a guard stays out of sight and whether that window is long enough to cross. Use hiding spots as steps in your plan, not as panic buttons. New devices often look harmless at first; test their range from a safe distance. If a route keeps failing, look for another way through a different floor.

Who should play it

Fits fans of the series and anyone who enjoys calm stealth puzzles. The new locations keep it fresh. It plays much like the earlier chapters, so newcomers can start here without missing much.
