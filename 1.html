1 – The 2048 rules, run in reverse

Take the sliding-tile puzzle everyone knows and flip its central rule. Here two matching tiles do not double when they collide. They merge into a tile worth half as much. The grid feels exactly like 2048, but your instincts now point the wrong way, and the target sits at the bottom of the number line.

The basics of 1

Every move pushes all tiles as far as they can travel in one direction. When two tiles with the same value touch, they combine into one tile at half that value, so two 64s become a 32. A new tile appears after each move. The aim is to halve your way down toward 1, the number in the title, without running out of empty cells.

Controls

  • Arrow / WASD Keys – move Number Tiles

Getting started

  • Make a few slow moves and watch which values the new tiles bring.
  • Pick one corner and keep your biggest tile parked there.
  • Merge large pairs early, since they clog the board fastest.
  • Check that a move leaves open cells before you press the key.

Tips from experience

Old 2048 habits still help if you reverse the logic. Line tiles up in a chain from big to small so each merge sets up the next. Do not swipe in all four directions at random; it scatters the values. Use two main directions and a third only when stuck. On a full board, pick the move that frees the most cells.

Who should play it

Good for 2048 players who want their autopilot broken. Rounds are short and the twist is easy to grasp. It is still a number puzzle at heart, so anyone tired of sliding tiles will find little new here.
