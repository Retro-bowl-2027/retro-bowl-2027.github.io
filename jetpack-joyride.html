Jetpack Joyride Online

Jetpack Joyride – Hold to fly, release to drop, dodge the zappers

An unlucky salesman named Barry Steakfries grabs a prototype jetpack and blasts out through a lab wall. That is the setup for Halfbrick's 2011 runner, one of the most recognizable in the genre, and this browser version keeps the essentials. Press to climb, release to sink, scoop up coins and thread past electric barriers, lasers and incoming missiles.

The basics of Jetpack Joyride

Barry keeps running without input. Hold the button and the jetpack pushes him upward; let go and he drops. Crackling barriers block sections of the corridor, laser beams cross the screen after a short warning, and missiles arrive after an alert flashes at the edge. Coins and pickups dot the route. Usually one hit is enough to end it.

Getting started

  • Use quick taps to hover rather than long presses.
  • When a missile alert flashes, move off the height it marks.
  • Leave any row where a laser beam begins to glow.
  • Take coins only when no hazard sits along the way.

Tips from experience

Altitude is controlled by rhythm, not by holding. Learn the tells: the missile marker reveals its height and the lasers light up before they fire. Shift position early, then stay put. Coins are a distraction from distance, and distance is the score. A tidy run beats a greedy one.

Who should play it

A classic for anyone who likes simple one-button runners with polish. It is quick to start. This is a browser version of a mobile hit, so some features may differ from the app.
