Handshakes – Make the right connections in a calm puzzle

Pet Pumpkin's Unity puzzle builds its challenges around a gentle idea: making the right connections. Handshakes gives you small puzzles where pieces need to meet in the correct way, and figuring out how they relate is the whole game. There is no timer pushing you. Take each puzzle at your own pace.

The basics of Handshakes

Each puzzle presents a set of pieces that need to link up. Study how they relate, decide the order of your actions and carry it out. A wrong move usually just means rethinking the arrangement. Later puzzles add more pieces and stricter conditions, so the connections become harder to see at first glance.

Getting started

  • Look over every piece before making the first move.
  • Ask which connection must happen first for the rest to work.
  • Try small, reversible moves when you are unsure.
  • Step back and rethink when a puzzle seems impossible.

Tips from experience

Work backward from the goal. Picture the finished arrangement, then figure out the last step, then the one before it. Puzzles like this often have one key connection that unlocks everything else; look for the piece with the fewest options. Patience works better than rapid trial and error, and the calm pace rewards it.

Who should play it

Good for players who enjoy relaxed logic puzzles without pressure. The tone is calm, and it suits short, quiet sessions. Experienced puzzlers may move through it quickly.
