Solitaire – Patience, one card at a time, up to four kings

Deal, sort, and clear the table. This HTML5 Solitaire is the patience game that shipped with so many old office computers, now running in a browser tab. Seven piles of cards, a draw pile and four empty home spots wait at the start. Get every card home, each suit climbing from its lowest card to its highest, and the round is yours.

The basics of Solitaire

On the seven piles, each card you place must be one rank lower than the card it covers and of the opposite color. Only cards showing their faces can be moved, and lifting one off a hidden card turns that card over. When the piles stall, flip cards from the draw pile until something fits. A home spot opens with an ace and grows one rank at a time within that suit.

Getting started

  • Send aces and twos home the moment they turn up.
  • Favor moves that reveal a hidden card in one of the seven piles.
  • Fill an emptied pile only with a king that can head a new run.
  • Turn to the draw pile only when the table has no useful move left.

Tips from experience

Dig into the tallest hidden piles first; they bury the cards you need most. Do not rush mid-value cards home while they still help you build on the table. Keep red and black options open for building. Think a move ahead before drawing, because some deals punish careless flips.

Who should play it

Ideal for players who want a calm, familiar card game. Rounds take a few minutes. A share of deals cannot be won no matter how well you play, which can sting after a long game.
