Galaga Online

Galaga – Hold the bottom line against swooping alien swarms

A single fighter at the bottom of the screen, a sky full of alien formations and no retreat. This unofficial Flash adaptation, credited to Robotube Studios with a 2004 date, recreates the famous arcade shooter. Aliens gather in neat rows, then peel away to swoop at you. Shoot them down before they reach you.

The basics of Galaga

Move left and right along the bottom and fire upward. Aliens arrive in groups and settle into formation, then break off one or several at a time to attack in curving runs. Getting hit costs a life. Clear every alien to advance to the next stage, where attacks come faster and from more angles.

Getting started

  • Stay near the center at the start so you can move either way.
  • Shoot aliens while they are still flying into formation.
  • Slip sideways away from swooping attackers rather than racing them.
  • Keep firing steadily even while you slide to a new position.

Tips from experience

Take out attackers before they commit. Aliens entering the screen follow predictable paths, so line up beneath them and fire early. When one breaks from the formation, track its curve and slide out of its way instead of trying to outshoot it. Clear the edges of the formation first; aliens from the sides often attack at awkward angles.

Who should play it

A good pick for fans of classic arcade shooters who want a quick fix. The controls are simple. As an unofficial Flash version, it may differ from the original arcade game in details and feel.
