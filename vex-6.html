Vex 6 Online

Vex 6 – The latest acts for patient stickman runners

New obstacles and challenges arrive in Vex 6, released in October 2021. The series' movement is unchanged: run, jump, slide and dodge through acts full of hazards. Checkpoints hold your place, and coins pay off careful play. Fans will feel at home, and the fresh hazards keep things challenging.

The basics of Vex 6

Work through each act by combining jumps, slides and dodges. Hazards include spikes, saws and moving traps, often arranged in combinations that demand quick switches between moves. Checkpoints break long acts into manageable sections. Collect coins for completion and unlock more acts as you go. A mistake sends you back to the last checkpoint.

Controls

  • Up Arrow – jump
  • Down Arrow – slide / Dodge
  • Left / Right Arrows – move Left / Right
  • Note – wASD keys also work

Getting started

  • Learn the new hazards slowly before speeding up.
  • Use checkpoints as places to practice difficult sections.
  • Combine jumps and slides to pass tight gaps.
  • Collect coins only when they sit on a safe route.

Tips from experience

Timing beats speed. Watch each trap's pattern before moving through it. Use slides to pass low hazards quickly and keep momentum. Practice from checkpoints until sections feel natural. Take breaks after long strings of failures, because tired reactions repeat the same mistakes.

Who should play it

Good for Vex veterans and fans of precise platformers. It keeps the series' style. The challenge stays high, so newcomers should expect plenty of retries.
