Super Mario 64 – Explore 3D worlds and collect stars

Big 3D worlds, shiny stars and a hero who can string jumps together. This Super Mario 64 is an unofficial browser game modeled on Nintendo's landmark 3D platformer. Wander open stages, scale walls and ledges, and hunt for stars, with coins scattered everywhere. Nintendo owns the name and characters; this is a fan-made version.

The basics of Super Mario 64

Each world is an open 3D space with platforms, enemies and secrets. Run, leap and climb to reach new areas. Stars are what count for progress, and coins pile up along the way. Linking two or three jumps in a row lifts you to ledges that a single hop cannot reach. Poking into every corner turns up more to find.

Getting started

  • Spend time on the first world to get used to the camera and jumps.
  • Practice chaining jumps on flat ground before tricky platforms.
  • Explore off the main path to find hidden stars and coins.
  • Adjust the camera often to judge distances accurately.

Tips from experience

Camera control is half the challenge. Rotate it before difficult jumps so you can judge distance. Learn which jump combinations give the most height. Grab coins when convenient, but focus on stars for progress. Search each world thoroughly before moving on to the next one.

Who should play it

Great for players who enjoy 3D platformers and exploration. It is unofficial, so features and performance may differ from the original game. Browser controls may feel different from a gamepad.
