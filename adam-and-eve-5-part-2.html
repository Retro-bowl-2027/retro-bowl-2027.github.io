Adam and Eve 5 Part 2 Online

Adam and Eve 5 Part 2 – The second half of Adam's search

Part 2 picks up where the first half stopped. Adam is still searching, the screens are still full of trouble, and Functu Games keeps the cheerfully clumsy humor the series is known for. Finish Part 1 first if you want the story in order. The puzzles themselves stand on their own, so you can also start here.

The basics of Adam and Eve 5 Part 2

The mechanics carry over unchanged. Every scene blocks Adam's way, and clicking the right objects opens a route forward. Some objects react once, while others change each time you click them. Get Adam to the far side of each scene and the next one loads. Reach the final screen to see whether he finally finds Eve.

Controls

  • Click – action

Getting started

  • Play Part 1 first so the setting and tone make sense.
  • Scan the full scene before your first click.
  • Click objects more than once, since some change with each click.
  • Retry a scene with a new order of clicks if Adam ends up stuck.

Tips from experience

Use what Part 1 taught you. Background items are rarely decoration, and the obvious object is often the second step, not the first. Before clicking, guess what each item could do to Adam's path, then test that guess. When you are stuck, look for something you have not clicked yet, however small it is. Slow, deliberate clicking solves scenes faster than rapid guessing.

Who should play it

Best for players who enjoyed Part 1 and want the rest of the story. It is short and gentle. There is little new in the mechanics, so anyone who found the first half too easy will feel the same here.
