10 Minutes Till Dawn Online

10 Minutes Till Dawn – Hold out against the horde until sunrise

Ten minutes sounds short. It stops feeling that way around minute four, when the darkness fills with eyes. This survival shooter by flanne gives you a gun, a small circle of light and one job: stay alive until the clock hits zero and dawn breaks. Monsters close in from every direction, and each minute brings more of them, moving faster.

The basics of 10 Minutes Till Dawn

Move with WASD and aim and fire with the mouse. Your gun holds limited shots, so reloading with R is part of the rhythm. Fallen monsters drop experience, and every level-up offers a choice of upgrades for your shots, speed or defenses. The run ends when you die or when ten minutes pass.

Controls

  • WASD Keys – move
  • Click – shoot
  • R – reload
  • Esc – pause

Getting started

  • Start with the default character and weapon to learn the pace of a run.
  • Keep moving in wide circles instead of standing still to shoot.
  • Collect experience drops quickly, before the crowd closes the gap.
  • Pick upgrades that support one plan, such as faster fire or extra projectiles.
  • Reload during quiet moments, not when enemies are already on top of you.

Tips from experience

Treat movement as your first defense and shooting as your second. Kite the horde around the map so it trails behind you in a line. Commit to one upgrade path; a run with five half-built ideas usually dies in the second half. Give larger enemies extra room. Press Esc to pause and read the upgrade text when you are unsure.

Who should play it

Fits players who like short roguelite runs with a clear finish line. Each attempt takes ten minutes or less, which suits a lunch break. It asks for steady mouse aim, so trackpad users will struggle.
