Save the Doge Online

Save the Doge – Draw a shield, survive the bees

One dog, one angry hive and a pen with limited ink. Save the Doge asks you to sketch a barrier before the bees are released, then hope it holds while they swarm. The countdown is short but tense. Placement and ink use decide each level, and a sloppy line can collapse at the worst moment.

The basics of Save the Doge

Every level starts paused with the dog, the hive and some scenery in view. Sketch a barrier with the mouse. When you let go, physics kicks in: the line can drop, roll or lean, and the bees start probing for a gap. Keep the dog untouched until the timer hits zero to clear the stage. An ink meter caps how much you can sketch.

Getting started

  • Find the hive and trace the route the bees will take before sketching.
  • Wrap the dog in a closed shape so no side is left open.
  • Rest the barrier on the ground or a ledge so it cannot slide off.
  • Keep lines short and deliberate to save ink for gaps.

Tips from experience

Gravity is the hidden enemy. An unsupported barrier will drop away from the dog. Lean shapes against walls and solid objects. Closed loops beat single lines. When a plan fails, move the barrier before redesigning it; position fixes more levels than shape.

Who should play it

Good for casual puzzle players and anyone who likes sketch-based solutions. Stages take seconds. Some later stages need several tries, which may annoy players who want every answer to come quickly.
